A day inside

What happens after the bio link goes up.

Saturday at the studio. Doors open at 10. Here’s what BookReady does in the background.

  1. 9:14 AM

    A new client books a French gel set. Deposit charged automatically. Confirmation email lands in their inbox.

  2. 9:32 AM

    Someone tries to book a slot you’ve blocked. Smart calendar says no, offers your waitlist instead. They’re queued.

  3. 10:08 AM

    11 AM cancels. Waitlist auto-fires. Three texts go out. Sixty seconds later, the slot is rebooked.

  4. 2:47 PM

    You post a $90 "After Hours" premium slot. Charged at booking. Gone in eight minutes.

  5. 7:55 PM

    Last client walks out. Review request emails fire an hour later. You moderate the ones that land on your site.

Common questions

Everything else you’d ask.

How fast can I really go live?

20 minutes if you’re focused. Pick a template, add 1-3 services with prices, hit publish. Most studios go from signup to a real booking in an afternoon.

Do I need any tech background?

No. There’s no code anywhere. The editor is point-and-click. If you can use Instagram, you can use BookReady.

Can I move from GlossGenius / Acuity / Booksy / Fresha?

Yes. We import your services, your client list, and your photos. See the migration guide →

What if my brand doesn’t fit any template?

Every template is owner-pickable: colors, fonts, headings, tab labels. Switch templates anytime without losing services, customers, or bookings. Have a mood we don’t cover yet? Email templates@bkrdy.com with a reference, we’ll build it.

Will BookReady take a cut of my bookings?

No. 0% platform markup. Stripe’s standard processing fee is the only deduction. The same fee they charge everywhere.
