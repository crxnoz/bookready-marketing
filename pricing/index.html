Questions

Pricing, answered.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Every new studio starts with 30 days of Solo, free, with no card. After that you can keep Solo for $1 the first month, or stay on the Free plan for good with nothing to cancel. A card only comes in when you pick a paid plan.

What happens after my 30 days of Solo?

Nothing is charged and nothing is deleted. If you don’t pick a plan, your studio moves to Free: your site, your calendar and your bookings keep working. Text reminders stop and email reminders keep going, and your site shows your first 6 gallery photos. Pick Solo any time and the rest comes back.

How does the $1 first month work?

The first time you pick Solo on monthly billing, your first month is $1. After that it is $21 a month, and you can cancel any time before then. It is once per studio. Studio and Studio+ start with 7 days free instead, with a card.

Does BKRDY take a cut of my payments?

No. Payments go to your own Stripe account. Stripe charges its standard US rate, 2.9% + 30¢ per card payment, and BKRDY adds nothing on top.

How does yearly billing work?

Same plan, lower rate, billed once a year: Solo is $216 ($18 a month), Studio $348 ($29 a month) and Studio+ $564 ($47 a month). You can switch between monthly and yearly whenever you like.

Will my price go up?

These are founding-member prices. Your rate stays the same for as long as your subscription stays active, even when prices rise for new customers.

Can I change plans later?

Any time, in both directions. Upgrades take effect right away and are prorated. Downgrades apply at your next billing date. Your site, services and clients stay as they are.

Is BKRDY available outside the US?

Not yet. Payments run on Stripe US, so BKRDY is for US businesses for now. More countries are on the roadmap.
