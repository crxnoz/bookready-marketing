Questions

Pricing, answered.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

You pick a plan and your card is charged. If you don’t want to continue, you can cancel from your dashboard and your site stays live for 30 days while you export your data.

What about transaction fees?

BookReady doesn’t take any. You pay Stripe’s standard rate (2.9% + 30¢ in the US) and that’s it. Deposits and full payments go straight to your Stripe account.

What’s the difference between monthly and annual?

Annual billing is the same plan at roughly two months free — Solo drops to $19/mo, Studio to $39/mo. You’re billed once a year and can switch back to monthly whenever.

What if I run out of SMS in a month?

You’ll get an email at 80% used. On Studio and Salon, additional messages are auto-billed at $0.025 each (with a hard cap you set). On Solo, you can buy a top-up pack or upgrade, whichever costs less.

Can I change plans later?

Any time, in both directions. Upgrades are prorated and take effect immediately. Downgrades apply at your next billing date. Your site, services, and clients stay exactly as they are.

Is BookReady built for the US?

Right now, yes. SMS is 10DLC US-only, payments are Stripe US, and the booking flow assumes US business hours. International expansion is on the roadmap.
