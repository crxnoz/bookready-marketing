How switching works

Questions

Short answers.

Can I take a deposit on a haircut?

Yes. Set one deposit rule, a flat amount or a percent of the price, and every booking asks for it. With a percent, a fade and beard asks for more than a line-up. It goes to your own Stripe account, and BKRDY takes no cut.

I rent a chair in someone else's shop. Does that work?

Yes. You get your own booking site, your own client book and your own Stripe account, so your clients and your payments stay yours if you change shops.

Do you handle walk-ins?

BKRDY books appointments, so there is no walk-in queue. Your weekly hours and time off decide what clients can book, and anything outside them stays free for the door.

Can clients book a specific barber?

Yes. Clients choose their barber, then see that barber's services and open times. On Studio and Studio+, each barber gets a login for their own schedule.

Does it connect to Instagram?

Your booking link works as your bio link, and the site loads fast from an Instagram tap. There is no direct Instagram integration.

I'm on Booksy. How do I switch?

We move your services, clients and upcoming appointments for you, free, usually the same day. Booksy, Square, Vagaro and Acuity all work, and your clients keep booking while we do it.
