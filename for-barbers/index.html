Designed first

A booking site that looks like a real shop floor

A barbershop site should feel like the shop, not like a form. Three templates designed around how a barbershop reads. The Fade Room runs dark and neon, like a late-night studio. Blackline reads architectural, with brushed-brass detailing and crisp modern lines. Bottega is the warm cream option, with terracotta accents and a villa feel. Pick the one that matches your room and the booking is already wired in underneath.

Per-chair calendars

Every chair, every barber, their own calendar

Each chair gets its own calendar. Every barber sets their own hours, services, and policies, and only sees their own bookings. Clients pick the barber they want when they book. No double-bookings, no calls about who is working today. If someone calls out, you re-route to another chair in two taps.

Deposits that hold

Deposits, no-show fees, and rules built into the flow

Stripe-backed deposits at booking time. Your cancellation window, late-cancel fee, and no-show fee all enforced on the booking flow, not on a paper sign at the door. The clients who showed up have already paid for the chair. Solo barbers tend to see no-show rates cut roughly in half in the first month.

Walk-ins, your way

Waitlist and same-day openings, automated

A live waitlist clients can join from their phone. Cancellations auto-fill from the list. Last-minute openings post to your booking link, and you can ping the waitlist by SMS so a slot fills in minutes, not hours. Set the radius and the time window, and the rest runs on its own.

Gallery built in

Photos that earn the next booking

Show your work where it matters. The gallery loads fast on mobile, supports before-and-after toggles for fades and beard sculpts, and links each photo back to the matching service in your menu. A client who taps a fade they like lands on the booking flow for that exact cut.

We import your services, hours, staff, and client list. Usually the same day. You keep every email, phone number, and standing appointment. It is free and included on every plan, so you are not paying a setup fee or losing a month while you switch.

Questions

The short answers for barbers.

Do I need a separate POS?

No. Stripe handles checkout for deposits and tips on every booking. If you sell product, you can add a basic catalog with quick-add buttons. Card-present tap-to-pay through your phone is on the roadmap.

Can each barber control their own schedule?

Yes. On Studio plan and up, each barber gets their own login, sets their own hours and services, and only sees their own calendar.

What about booth renters?

Each booth renter can run their own setup inside the shop’s site, with their own deposits and payouts going straight to their own Stripe account.

Can clients book a specific barber?

Yes. Clients pick the barber first, then see only that person’s services and openings. If they don’t care, "any barber" lets the system pick the next opening.

What if I don’t want walk-ins?

Just don’t enable the waitlist. The booking flow only shows scheduled openings, and same-day slots are off by default.

What does it cost to switch?

Zero to migrate. Then $24/mo (Solo) or $49/mo (Studio) on your 14-day free trial. No card needed for the first 7 days. Cancel anytime.

Do I keep my domain?

Yes. Plug your domain into BookReady on Studio plan and up. Or use a clean bkrdy.me subdomain free, like thefade.bkrdy.me.

Does the booking handle gift cards?

Gift cards and pre-paid packages ship in late 2026. For now, the booking flow handles services, add-ons, deposits, and tips.
