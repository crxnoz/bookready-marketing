What to do first.

Your Dashboard keeps a short setup list. Here is the order we suggest.

01

Add your services.

Open Calendar, then Services. Give each one its length, price and a photo, and add any buffer time you need before or after.

02

Set your hours.

Open Calendar, then Availability. Lay out your usual week, add any time off, and decide how far ahead clients can book.

03

Write your policies.

Open Settings, then Policies. Set your cancellation and reschedule windows and say what clients should know before they come in. You can ask them to tick an "I agree" box when they book.

04

Connect Stripe.

Open Settings, then Payment rules, and connect your own Stripe account. Then set your deposit, a flat amount or a percent of the price. BKRDY takes 0% commission.

05

Share your link.

If the editor asks you to confirm your email, do that first so your site can go public. Book yourself once to see what clients see, then put your link in your bio.
