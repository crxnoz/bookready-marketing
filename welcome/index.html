What's next

Five steps to live.

Work through these in order. Most studios are taking real bookings the same day.

1. Pick your template

Seven designed looks, not blank canvases. Choose the one that already feels like you, then make it yours.

2. Add your services and prices

Your menu becomes your booking page. Set prices, durations, and which services need a deposit.

3. Connect Stripe

Take payments, deposits, and tips straight to your bank, with 0% markup on top of Stripe.

4. Connect your domain

Point your own domain at your site, or use a clean free one to start. Either works.

5. Share your link

Drop it in your Instagram bio and your Google profile. That is the moment the bookings start.

That's it

No plugins, no developer, no weekend lost. A real site that does the booking for you.
