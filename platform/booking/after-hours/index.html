Questions

Short answers.

Can the fee be a percentage?

No. Both fees are a flat dollar amount you set. A squeeze-in time can carry its own fee or use your default, and the client sees the fee before they book.

Can I open after hours only on some days?

Yes. Pick the weekdays you want, and switch a single date off from that day's settings when you need the night back. You also set how far past close you'll go and the latest start.

Can clients request a squeeze-in?

No. You publish squeeze-in times yourself for a date, and clients book them directly at the fee. When a day is full, the waitlist is how clients ask to get in.

Can I keep late slots for VIPs only?

Yes. Set who can book to everyone, existing clients or VIP only. Mark a client as VIP from their profile in your client book.

Is there a limit on after-hours bookings?

Set a nightly cap and the extra times stop once it's reached. You also choose the latest start time, so nobody can book a start later than you want.

Is this on the free plan?

No. After hours and squeeze-ins come with Solo, Studio and Studio+. Every new studio gets 30 days of Solo free, with no card, so you can try them first.
