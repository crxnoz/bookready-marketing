Good to know

Sync questions.

Which calendars can I connect?

The big ones, Google, Apple (iCloud), and Outlook. Connect the calendar you already check daily.

Does it go both ways?

Yes. Bookings flow into your calendar, and busy times in your calendar block those slots from being booked.

Will clients see my personal events?

Never. They only see that a slot is unavailable, never what’s on your personal calendar.

Why it matters

Stop checking two calendars.

If your bookings live in one place and the rest of your life in another, a collision is only a matter of time, a dentist appointment booked right over a client.

Connecting your calendar makes BookReady the single source of truth: everything that takes your time is in one view, and busy is busy everywhere.
