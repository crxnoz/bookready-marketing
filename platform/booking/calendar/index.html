Good to know

Calendar questions.

Can each staff member have their own hours?

Yes. Everyone gets their own working hours, services, days off, and buffers, clients only see real openings for the person they pick.

Can it prevent back-to-back bookings?

Add a buffer to a service and the calendar automatically leaves cleanup or travel time around it.

What stops two people booking the same slot?

A slot locks the instant it’s taken, so a second client never sees it, no double-bookings.
