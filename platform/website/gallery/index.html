Questions

Short answers.

How many photos can I show?

On Free, 6 gallery photos. On Solo, Studio and Studio+, up to 3 collections of 6 photos each. Before-and-after pairs go in the Results section on every plan.

Can I upload from my phone?

Yes. Select several photos at once from your camera roll. JPEG, PNG, WebP and iPhone HEIC photos all work, so there is nothing to convert first.

Can I hide a photo without deleting it?

Yes. A hidden photo leaves your public site but stays in your editor, ready to show again with one tap.

Do my photos stay if I switch templates?

Yes. Your gallery is kept when you change templates, and the new template shows the same photos in its own style.

Can BKRDY pull my photos from Instagram?

No. BKRDY doesn’t import from Instagram. Upload the photos you want to show, or add visit photos straight from your appointments.
