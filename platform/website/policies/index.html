Questions

Short answers.

Where do clients see my policies?

On the Policy tab of your site, which you can rename, like House rules. Confirmation emails also remind clients that your cancellation policy applies.

Does BKRDY enforce my cancellation window?

Yes, for changes clients make themselves. Inside your window, the link in their email won’t cancel or reschedule, and it tells them how much notice you need.

Can I charge for late cancellations?

On every plan you can take a deposit at booking and choose to keep it on a late cancel. On paid plans you can also set a late cancellation fee and charge a card saved with the client’s consent.

Can I have different policies per service?

No. One set of policies covers your whole business, and so does your deposit rule. Extra notes or a custom group can call out anything specific to one service.

Do I have to write them from scratch?

Each field opens with a prompt, like how much notice clients need to give, so you know what to cover. The words are yours, which keeps them true to how you work.
