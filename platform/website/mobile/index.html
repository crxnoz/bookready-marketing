Questions

Short answers.

Do clients need an app to book?

No. Clients book from your site in their phone’s browser, as a guest. A free client account is there for anyone who wants their bookings and receipts in one place.

Do I have to set anything up for phones?

No. Every template is built to work on a phone as it comes, so there is nothing to resize or rearrange for small screens.

Can clients send photos from their phone?

On paid plans, yes. Your booking form can ask for photos, like inspiration pics or their current nails, and clients attach them straight from their camera roll.

What are theme effects?

A light layer of floating hearts, sparkles, petals, stars or holiday shapes that drifts over your site on phones only. It stays off until you pick one, and it comes with paid plans or the $5.99 a month add-on.

Can I run things from my phone too?

Yes. The BKRDY editor works in your phone’s browser, so you can check today’s bookings, answer requests and update your site between clients.
