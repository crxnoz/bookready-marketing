Questions

Short answers.

How many templates are there?

Fifteen. The Free plan includes four: Opaline, Blackline, The Fade Room and Lush Studio. Solo, Studio and Studio+ include all 15, and Free sites can open all 15 with the $5.99 a month design add-on.

What happens to my site when I switch templates?

Your services, hours, gallery, clients and bookings stay exactly as they are. Settings that belong to a template, like colors, tab names, section order and your About and Advice copy, reset to the new one.

Can I change colors and fonts?

Each template has its own set of color choices for its accent or page background. Fonts and layout come with the template, which is what keeps every site looking designed.

Can I build my own layout from scratch?

No. BKRDY isn’t a blank page builder. You start from a finished design and make it yours with your words, your photos, your colors and the order of your tabs.

Can I remove the Powered by BKRDY badge?

Free sites show a small Powered by BKRDY badge, even with the design add-on. On Solo, Studio and Studio+ you can turn it off in your footer settings.
