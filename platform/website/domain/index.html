Good to know

Domain questions.

Can I use a domain I already own?

Yes, on Studio and Salon. Point it at BookReady and we’ll walk you through the DNS records, it takes a few minutes.

What do I get on Solo?

A clean BookReady subdomain like yourname.bkrdy.me. You can connect a custom domain later by upgrading, without rebuilding your site.

Is HTTPS included?

Always. Every site gets an SSL certificate automatically, and we renew it for you, clients always see the padlock.

Why it matters
