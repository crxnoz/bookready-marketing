Questions

Short answers.

Can I add my own sections?

No. Every template keeps the same 12 sections, which is what keeps each one designed. You can rename the tabs, hide the ones you don’t use, reorder them, and add your own groups inside the policy section, like parking or aftercare.

Which sections can I hide?

Any of them except the header, the booking section and the footer. Those three stay on so your site always has a way to book. Hidden sections keep their content for when you bring them back.

What does the Sample text badge mean?

It marks a section whose words still match the template’s sample copy. Write your own and the badge goes away, so you can see at a glance what is left to make yours.

Are the FAQ and thank-you sections on Free?

No. They come with Solo, Studio and Studio+, or with the $5.99 a month design add-on on Free. The reviews section shows on every plan, including Free.

Do my tab names survive a template switch?

No. Tab names, section order and your About and Advice copy reset to the new template. Your services, hours, gallery, clients and bookings stay as they are.
