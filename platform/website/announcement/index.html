Questions

Short answers.

Is the announcement bar on the Free plan?

Not on its own. It’s on Solo, Studio and Studio+, and Free sites can add it with the $5.99 a month design add-on, along with every template and the introduction block.

How long can the message be?

Up to 200 characters. Short works best: one fact a client can read at a glance, like your holiday hours or a new service.

Can I show more than one message?

Two. Add a promotion and the bar rotates between it and your main message on its own. One promotion runs at a time.

Can it pop up instead?

No. The announcement bar is a line across the top of your site, not a pop-up, so it never gets between a client and the booking button.

Will it text or email my clients?

No. The bar only shows on your site. BKRDY doesn’t send marketing texts or email blasts, only booking confirmations and reminders.
