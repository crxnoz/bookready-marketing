Good to know

Reminder questions.

Why do US texts need 10DLC?

It’s the US carrier standard for business texting. We register your number so reminders land reliably instead of getting filtered.

Do reminders count against my SMS quota?

Reminders use your plan’s SMS allowance; confirmations and verification codes don’t count. The pricing page has an SMS calculator.

Can clients reschedule from a reminder?

Yes, every reminder carries a link to reschedule within the policy you set.
