Good to know

Payment questions.

Does BookReady take a cut of bookings?

No. We charge your plan fee and nothing on transactions. You pay only Stripe’s standard processing rate.

Where does the money go?

Straight into your own Stripe account on Stripe’s normal payout schedule. We never hold your funds.

Can I take a deposit instead of full payment?

Yes, set a deposit amount per service to hold the slot, or take payment in full. Your choice.

Why it matters

No middleman markup.

A lot of booking platforms quietly skim a percentage of every appointment on top of card fees. Over a busy month, that adds up to real money out of your pocket.

BookReady doesn’t take a cent of your payments. You connect your own Stripe account, pay Stripe’s standard rate, and keep the rest, deposits and balances land straight in your balance.
