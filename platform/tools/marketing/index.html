Questions

Short answers.

Does BKRDY send email or text campaigns?

No. BKRDY does not send marketing emails or texts to your client list. The Content studio makes posts you share yourself, and reminders go out for each booking.

Does it post to Instagram for me?

No. You download the image or share it from your phone, then post it yourself. There is no Instagram connection to set up, and nothing posts without you.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. My work cards use photos from your gallery, up to four per card, and you can upload your own backgrounds under Brand assets to use on other cards.

Do the cards update when my prices change?

A new card reads your current menu, hours and reviews when you make it. An image you already downloaded stays exactly as it was.

Which plans include the Content studio?

Solo, Studio and Studio+. It is not part of the Free plan or the $5.99 design add-on for Free sites.
