Questions

Short answers.

How many staff can I add?

Up to 6 on Studio ($34/mo) and up to 12 on Studio+ ($55/mo). Free and Solo have one calendar. For bigger teams, Salon is priced per project, so talk to us.

Does BKRDY run payroll or track commissions?

No. There is no payroll, commission or booth-rent tracking. The payments overview does show revenue by artist, which helps when you work out pay yourself.

Can each artist get paid into their own Stripe account?

No. All payments go to the studio's one Stripe account, with 0% commission to BKRDY. Splitting money between artists happens outside BKRDY.

What can a staff member see?

Their own schedule and appointments, plus their own hours and profile. Studio settings, payments and the rest of the business stay with you.

Can clients choose their artist?

Yes. Clients pick an artist who does the service they booked, or choose any available staff and get whoever is free at that time.

Do I pay extra for each artist?

No. Seats are included in your plan: Studio covers up to 6 staff and Studio+ up to 12, with no charge per artist.
