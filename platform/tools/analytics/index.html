Questions

Short answers.

Is there a separate reports section to set up?

No. The numbers live in the payments overview and on Home, and they fill in as clients book and pay. Soon Kito will answer questions about them in plain words.

Can I see where my bookings come from, like Instagram or Google?

No. BKRDY does not track site traffic or ad sources. The numbers here come from your own bookings and payments.

Does it track no-shows?

Yes. The No-show risk segment in your client book flags clients who often miss, and soon you can ask Kito how many appointments were missed or cancelled in a period.

Do cash payments count?

Yes. When you mark a balance paid in cash, Venmo or Zelle, it shows under payment methods and in your totals, next to your card payments.

Is it on the Free plan?

Yes. The payments overview and the CSV export work on every plan, including Free, and Kito will too when he arrives. Revenue by artist appears once you add a team on Studio or Studio+.
