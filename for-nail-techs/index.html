Manicure, gel, builder gel, extensions. Length tiers from short to coffin. Simple art, intricate art, design tiers. Each add-on shows the extra time and price as the client picks. They see the real total and the real duration before they hit book, so no awkward "actually that’s more" calls later.

Right-sized deposits

Deposits that match the time you’re holding

A 3-hour full set with extensions and art is too valuable to lose to a no-show. Set a flat deposit, a percentage of the service price, or a per-hour rate. Stripe handles it. The deposit becomes credit toward the appointment, so nothing feels punitive, but no-shows hurt much less.

How migration works

Questions

The short answers for nail techs.

Can clients pick a style from the gallery?

Yes. Tag each gallery photo to a service, and clients can tap a photo to start the booking pre-filled with that service. No more "the second one from the left" texts.

How do add-ons work?

Define add-ons per service with their own time and price. Clients pick the base service, then add length, art, or design tiers as needed. Real total and real duration always shown.

Can I take a custom deposit per service?

Yes. Deposit rules are per service: flat amount, percentage of price, or per-hour rate. Use a higher deposit on long extension sets and a lighter one on quick fills.

What about recurring fills?

Standing recurring appointments (e.g. every 3 weeks at 2 PM) are built in. Clients book once, and the appointments roll forward at their cadence until they cancel.

Can my clients reschedule themselves?

Yes, within the window you set, straight from the confirmation email or text. Outside the window, they have to call or message, so you keep control of late changes.

Will I lose my clients during migration?

No. Every name, email, phone number, and recurring appointment imports cleanly. Free, usually same-day. You keep your list.

Do I need a separate website?

This is the website. Your services, hours, gallery, policies, and booking all live here under your domain (Studio plan) or a free bkrdy.me subdomain.

Does it handle gift cards?

Gift cards ship in late 2026. The booking flow currently handles services, add-ons, deposits, tips, and recurring appointments.
