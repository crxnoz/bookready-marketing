How switching works

Questions

Short answers.

Can clients add nail art or length when they book?

Yes. Set up add-ons like length, art or chrome, each with its own price and time. Clients pick what they want, and the appointment is sized to match.

Can I take a deposit on full sets?

Yes. Set one deposit rule, a flat amount or a percent of the price, and every booking asks for it. With a percent, a three-hour set asks for more than a polish change. It goes to your own Stripe account, and BKRDY takes no cut.

Can clients send inspiration photos?

Yes, on paid plans. Clients attach photos to the booking, like inspo pics or their current nails, so you see what they want before they arrive.

Can clients cancel or move a fill themselves?

Yes, from the link in their confirmation email, inside the windows you set. Outside the window they have to ask you. Paid plans can add a late cancellation fee on a card on file.

Can I sell press-ons or care kits?

Yes. Add products like press-ons or care kits to your Shop. Clients reserve them online and pick them up from you, so there is nothing to ship.

I'm on another app. How do I switch?

We move your services, clients and upcoming appointments for you, free, usually the same day. We work from Booksy, Vagaro, GlossGenius, Square, Fresha, StyleSeat and Acuity.
