What’s included

Built for a busy chair. Not a brochure.

The Fade Room ships with the whole booking engine wired up. Add your cuts, your barbers, and your hours, clients book and pay a deposit from their phone in a few taps.

Deposits at booking

Take a deposit through Stripe before you hold the chair. Cuts no-shows on the busy days.

Per-barber booking

Each barber gets their own services, hours, and calendar. Clients book the chair they want.

SMS reminders

Confirmation on booking, reminder 24h before. Fewer empty slots, less back-and-forth.

Cuts gallery

Show your fades and line-ups in a built-in gallery so new clients know what they’re getting.

Announcement bar

Post walk-in hours, holiday closures, or last-minute openings with a single toggle up top.

Switch any time

Outgrow the look? Switch templates and keep every booking, client, barber, and policy.
