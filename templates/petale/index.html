The details

What makes Pétale.

Every template runs the same booking engine: services, deposits, reminders, policies and reviews. Only the look changes.

A scalloped tab underline.

The open tab gets a scalloped curve beneath it instead of a straight line, a soft edge borrowed from wedding stationery, with scalloped hairlines to match.

Script where it matters.

Pinyon Script appears only at the moments that matter, like your signature lines, so the flourish feels special instead of busy.

An asymmetric About.

Your About section pairs two photos in an asymmetric diptych, one set off from the other, for a layout that feels editorial and feminine at once.

Who picks it

Hair salons with a romantic brand, nail rooms doing soft pink and nude work, gentle lash studios, bridal makeup artists and hair-for-events stylists tend to pick Petale.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I change the colors?

Yes. Choose Blush, Rose Quartz, Peach, Mauve, Dusty Rose or Cream for the canvas. The accent shifts with it, gold on the light canvases and wine or copper on the deeper pinks.

Is it only for bridal work?

No. It suits any studio with a soft, feminine brand: salons, nail rooms and lash studios as much as bridal artists. Rename and reorder the tab sections to fit your services.

Can I switch later?

Yes. Change templates whenever you like from your editor. Services, clients and bookings stay put, so moving to a new look never disturbs a booked bride.
