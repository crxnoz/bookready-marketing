Built for soft brands

A romantic, feminine beauty template

Petale runs blush and rose with handwritten champagne-gold script. Romantic, airy, considered. Built for salons, lash studios, nail rooms, and makeup artists with a soft brand. The gold script ties through service names, section headers, and the booking flow. Reads like a brand, not a form.

On a phone

See it where your clients see it.

Already in the template

Every section a soft beauty brand needs

Hero with studio name and a romantic flourish. Service menu with add-ons and tiers. Per-stylist or per-artist bios. Gallery filterable by mood, color, or style. Intake forms with allergies and prep notes. Booking flow with deposits, recurring fills, and email reminders. All the gold accents stay consistent across the page.

Who tends to pick it

Soft, feminine beauty brands

Hair salons with a romantic brand. Nail rooms doing soft pink and nude work. Lash studios with a gentle aesthetic. Makeup artists with a bridal portfolio. Beauty studios that read feminine and want a site to match. Not a fit for dark, editorial work, that is Velvet Theory.
