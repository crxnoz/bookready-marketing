Built for clinical luxury

A medspa-ready home, photo-light by design.

Clarity runs off-white with a muted sage default and Inter throughout. Type-led, photo-light, generous whitespace. Built for medspas, fine-line and micro-tattoo studios, premium nail and lash artists who want to read luxury and clinical without being cold.

Already in the template

Every section a clinical studio needs

Editorial hero with studio name and a single high-key photo. Service menu styled like an Apple product page. Per-provider bios with credentials. Gallery with restrained, paper-margin crops. Intake notes, before-and-after, hours, policies, and a booking flow with deposits. All of it quiet, all of it considered.

Who tends to pick it

Studios with a clinical, luxury brand

Medspas. Fine-line and micro-tattoo studios that want to read editorial, not gritty. Premium nail and lash studios. Estheticians running series and packages. Wellness-adjacent practices that want a real booking site, not a clipboard. Not a fit for moody, jewel-toned brands, that is Velvet Theory.
