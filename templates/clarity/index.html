The details

What makes Clarity.

Every template runs the same booking engine: services, deposits, reminders, policies and reviews. Only the look changes.

A single status dot.

The open tab gains a small dot in your accent color beside its label, and the label darkens from gray to ink. A precise move instead of a bar.

Sharp, not rounded.

Corners stay square everywhere, buttons and booking flow included, because luxury reads sharp. Fine hairlines and paper margins keep everything ordered and calm.

Thin, big headlines.

Section titles run large in light Inter with small, tracked uppercase eyebrows above them, so the type does the work and photos never have to shout.

Who picks it

Medspas, fine-line and micro-tattoo studios, premium nail and lash studios, and estheticians who want to read clinical luxury without feeling cold tend to pick Clarity.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I change the accent?

Yes. Choose Sage, Dusty Rose, Mauve or Stone from your dashboard. Each one is a low, dusty tone, so dark text stays easy to read and the page keeps its calm.

Is it only for medspas?

No. It also suits micro-tattoo artists, nail and lash studios and estheticians. Every template carries the same 12 sections, and you can rename, reorder or hide the tabs you do not need.

Can I switch later?

Yes, any time. Your services, clients and bookings stay exactly as they are when you change templates, so the switch never touches your calendar.
