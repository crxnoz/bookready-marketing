✦ CHROMA STUDIO ✦

IBM Plex Mono

Built for the brand

A Chroma template that reads its mood without effort

Chroma is a Y2K iridescent storefront — conic-gradient orb diffused behind the wordmark, holographic foil on the headlines, chrome bubble pills on every CTA. Built for businesses whose finish is the product: colorists, chrome-nail artists, gloss treatments, glass-skin facialists.

Already in the template

Every section a Chroma studio needs

Hero with the wordmark over an animated conic orb, sticky tab rail with chrome pill buttons, gallery that pairs grid and strips, results / before-after laid out as foiled cards, FAQ accordion with pearl chrome dividers, reviews stacked as glossy testimonials, services menu that handles color tiers and add-ons, full booking flow with deposits and Stripe Connect, footer with the iridescent wordmark and quick-book CTA.

Who tends to pick it

Studios that match Chroma’s mood

Colorists, chrome-nail studios, gloss bars, holographic-nail artists, brow lifts, lash artists with pearlescent finishes, and any salon whose brand speaks Y2K. Not a fit for understated minimalist studios — those want Clarity.
