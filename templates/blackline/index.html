The details

What makes Blackline.

Every template runs the same booking engine: services, deposits, reminders, policies and reviews. Only the look changes.

Brass hairline rules.

Thin brushed-brass rules divide the page, and the same brass carries the buttons and active states, like good hardware on a well-made door.

Sharp corners, tracked caps.

Square corners everywhere and tracked uppercase eyebrows give the page an editorial, architectural feel instead of a nostalgic one. The brass does most of the talking.

Booking reads like a chapter.

A single-column editorial rhythm runs top to bottom, so the booking section sits in the page like a chapter of its own, not a widget dropped in.

Who picks it

Barbershops with a design eye, men's grooming studios in office districts that want to read professional rather than nocturnal, and boutique beauty studios doing minimalist work.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I change the colors?

Yes. Choose Onyx, Charcoal, Walnut or Bone for the canvas from your dashboard. The brushed brass stays constant and the fonts come with the template, so every version still reads as Blackline.

Is it only for barbers?

No. It suits any studio that wants a sleek, minimal look, from grooming lounges to brow and nail studios. Rename and reorder the tab sections, and hide the ones you do not use.

Can I switch later?

Yes, whenever you like. Your services, clients and bookings carry over to the new template, so a fresh look never costs you your book.
