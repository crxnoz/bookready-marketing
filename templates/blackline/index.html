Built with restraint

A barbershop template for architectural rooms

Blackline runs heavy black canvas with brushed-brass detailing and crisp typography. Designed for shops that read architectural, not editorial. Boutique barbershops, men’s grooming studios with a design-forward room, and beauty studios doing minimalist work. Less neon than The Fade Room, more discipline.

On a phone

See it where your clients see it.

Already in the template

Every section a restrained shop needs

Hero with shop name and a tight benefit tagline. Service menu with prices and durations, presented in a single line per service. Per-chair or per-staff calendars. Gallery with restrained crops and minimal captions. Hours, policies, and a clean booking flow. The brushed-brass accent runs through buttons and active states.

Who tends to pick it

Shops that lead with discipline, not noise

Barbershops with a design background or a brand consultant on retainer. Men’s grooming studios in office districts that want to read professional, not nocturnal. Boutique beauty studios doing minimalist work. Not a fit for shops with a loud, after-hours vibe, that is The Fade Room.
