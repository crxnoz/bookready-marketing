The details

What makes Velvet Theory.

Every template runs the same booking engine: services, deposits, reminders, policies and reviews. Only the look changes.

A flush gold tab bar.

The open tab is marked by a flush gold bar, and the same warm gold runs through the buttons and rules on every canvas, so the page always feels finished.

An editorial subtitle.

Your tagline sits under your name in place of a business-type label, set like the cover line of a magazine over Fraunces headings.

Reserve, in the flow.

Instead of a floating quick-book pill, a Reserve call to action sits in the flow of the page, so booking feels like part of the story.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I change the colors?

Yes. Choose Burgundy, Midnight, Emerald, Plum or Charcoal for the canvas from your dashboard. The warm gold stays constant, and every canvas is dark by design, so the drama never drops.

Is it only for hair salons?

No. Lash studios, makeup artists and beauty boutiques with a luxe, editorial brand fit it well. Rename and reorder the tab sections to match your services and hide the ones you skip.

Can I switch later?

Yes, whenever you like. Your services, clients and bookings stay exactly as they are when you change templates, so the look can change while the calendar stays put.
