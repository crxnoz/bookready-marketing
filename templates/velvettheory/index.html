Built for editorial rooms

A salon template with editorial weight

Velvet Theory runs deep burgundy with constant warm gold accents. Dramatic, after-dark, considered. Built for hair salons with a strong color program, lash studios doing volume work, and makeup artists with an editorial portfolio. The gold accent ties every section together so the whole page reads as one brand.

Already in the template

Every section a luxe salon needs

Hero with studio name and an editorial subhead. Service menu with color tiers, processing time built in, and price-on-request options for custom work. Per-stylist or per-artist bios with portfolio links. Gallery with mood filters (color, lash, makeup) and before-and-after. Hours, policies, deposit terms. Booking flow with per-stylist calendars and Stripe deposits.

Who tends to pick it

Salons and lash studios that lead with brand

Hair salons doing color work as a signature service. Lash studios doing volume and mega-volume sets where the room reads luxe. Makeup artists with editorial work in their portfolio. Beauty boutiques with multiple service lines. Not a fit for soft, romantic brands, that is Petale.

Plan that fits

Studio at $29/mo covers most Velvet Theory salons

Studio covers a salon with multiple stylists or artists, per-staff calendars, separate Stripe payouts, color processing time, and a waitlist. Solo stylists and solo lash artists can run Velvet Theory on Solo ($15/mo) with a single calendar. Compare plans →
