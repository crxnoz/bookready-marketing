The details

What makes Opaline.

Every template runs the same booking engine: services, deposits, reminders, policies and reviews. Only the look changes.

A champagne wash on the tab.

The open tab sits in a soft champagne-wash pill instead of a bar or underline, the quiet signature that runs through the whole page.

Hairline rules, tracked caps.

Fine champagne hairlines divide the sections, and tracked uppercase Jost eyebrows sit over light Cormorant headings, like the label on a good bottle of oil.

Numbered timeline steps.

Your timeline section walks clients through the visit with elegant numerals in your accent color, so prep and arrival notes feel like part of the ritual.

Who picks it

Day spas, solo facialists and skincare studios, lash artists doing volume work in a quiet room, and wellness studios that care about calm tend to pick Opaline.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I change the champagne?

Yes. Choose Champagne, Taupe, Rose Nude, Sage Mist, Slate, Lilac Ash or Mineral Blue from your dashboard. Each one is low and soft, so the page reads premium whichever you pick.

Does it suit a medspa or waxing studio?

Yes. Its calm, bright look fits medspas, waxing studios, injectors and lash rooms as well as day spas. Rename and reorder the tab sections to match your treatments.

Can I switch later?

Any time. Your services, clients and bookings stay in place when you change templates, so you can refresh the look without touching a single appointment.
