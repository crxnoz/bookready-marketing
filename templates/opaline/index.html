Built for quiet rooms

A spa template that reads luxury without effort

Opaline runs pearl-and-marble with muted metallic accents. Spacious, elegant, restrained. Built for day spas, wellness studios, lash bars, and skincare rooms that want quiet luxury, not dramatic darkness. The whole page reads slow. The booking flow feels considered.

On a phone

See it where your clients see it.

Already in the template

Every section a wellness studio needs

Hero with studio name and a soft subhead. Service menu with multi-service packages, add-ons, and series pre-pays. Per-practitioner and per-room calendars (a booking can hold both). Gallery with restrained photography. Intake forms with allergies and contraindications. Pre-visit prep instructions sent with reminders. Booking flow with deposits sized for hour-plus services.

Who tends to pick it

Spas and treatment rooms with quiet luxury

Day spas with multiple practitioners and rooms. Solo facialists and skincare studios. Lash studios doing volume work in a quiet room. Wellness studios with a treatment-plan structure (series of facials, massages, body work). Not a fit for dramatic, after-dark brands, that is Velvet Theory.
