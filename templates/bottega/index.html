The details

What makes Bottega.

Every template runs the same booking engine: services, deposits, reminders, policies and reviews. Only the look changes.

A patterned background.

Bottega is the only template with a pattern behind every section: Ceramic mosaic by default, or Cherry Blossom, Leaves, Marble or Coastal, switched from your dashboard.

A side-by-side About.

Your About section shows two photos of equal weight side by side, a simple diptych that lets the studio and the work share the stage.

A speckle on the active tab.

The open tab is marked with a small three-dot speckle cluster, a tiny callback to the pattern that ties the menu to the rest of the page.

Who picks it

Hair salons with a Mediterranean or artisanal feel, handcrafted beauty brands, multi-service studios doing brows, lashes, nails and skin under one roof, and heritage barbershops tend to pick Bottega.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I change the pattern and colors?

Yes, both. Choose Ceramic, Cherry Blossom, Leaves, Marble or Coastal for the pattern, and Rust, Walnut, Slate, Deep Navy or Sage for the accent. The cream canvas and fonts stay fixed.

Does it suit a barbershop?

It can. Heritage barbershops like it for the warmth, and so do salons, nail bars and lash studios. Rename and reorder the tab sections so the site speaks your trade.

Can I switch later?

Yes. Swap templates whenever you like. Your services, clients and bookings stay in place, so changing the look never interrupts the people already on your calendar.
