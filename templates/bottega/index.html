Built for handcrafted brands

A warm, villa-inspired studio template

Bottega runs warm cream with terracotta-rust accents and a signature pattern (ceramic mosaic by default, with cherry blossom, leaves, marble, and coastal options). Built for beauty studios with a handcrafted, villa-inspired brand. The only template with a patterned background. Reads tactile, warm, and unhurried.

Already in the template

Every section a warm studio needs

Hero with studio name and a tactile photo. Pattern that runs across the whole page (toggle between five options in your dashboard). Service menu with cream-and-terracotta styling. Per-stylist or per-artist bios. Gallery with restrained crops. Hours, policies, and a booking flow with deposits. All of it warm, all of it considered.

Who tends to pick it

Studios with a warm, considered brand

Hair salons with a Mediterranean or artisanal feel. Beauty studios with a handcrafted brand. Multi-service studios doing brows, lashes, nails, and skincare under one warm roof. Barbershops with a heritage feel. Not a fit for sleek, minimalist brands, that is Blackline.
