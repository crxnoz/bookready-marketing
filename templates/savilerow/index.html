The details

What makes Savile Row.

Every template runs the same booking engine: services, deposits, reminders, policies and reviews. Only the look changes.

Type left, portrait right.

The hero splits in two: your name and details on the left, a tall portrait on the right, like a page from a tailor's fitting-room catalog.

A spec-sheet frame.

Your hero and About photos sit inside a tailor's spec-sheet frame, a double hairline with corner registration ticks, like a plate in an engraver's proof.

Canvases named for cloth.

Every canvas option comes from the same dark suiting family, so switching between them never breaks the look. The brass and bone ink hold steady throughout.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I change the colors?

Yes. Choose one of seven dark canvases named for suiting cloth, from Charcoal Flannel to Tobacco. Brass and bone stay constant and the fonts come with the template, so the shop keeps one identity.

Is it only for barbers?

It was cut for barbers and men's grooming, but any studio that wants a classic, tailored feel fits. Rename and reorder the tab sections so they match your services.

Can I switch later?

Yes. Switch templates any time and your services, clients and bookings stay put, so every barber's book stays exactly as it was.
