The details

What makes Arcanum.

Every template runs the same booking engine: services, deposits, reminders, policies and reviews. Only the look changes.

An engraved gold frame.

A gold rule with four corner ornaments frames the whole hero like a tarot card, and the active tab wears four small gold corner brackets to match it.

A celestial sigil on top.

A moon-and-stars sigil floats above your name, and vertical gold hairlines divide the wordmark, the tagline and the contact buttons like ceremonial pauses.

An illuminated About page.

Your About section reads as the studio's own page in the deck, opening with a large gold drop cap on the first letter of your story.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I change the colors?

Yes, the gold. Pick Antique, Champagne, Bronze, Pewter or Burnished from your dashboard and the frame, rules and sigil follow. The midnight indigo canvas and the fonts stay fixed, so the look holds together.

Does it work if I'm not a tarot reader?

Yes. It also suits facialists, lash and brow artists, colorists and tattoo studios with a ritual feel. Every template has the same 12 sections, and you can rename and reorder the tabs to fit your services.

Can I switch templates later?

Any time, from your editor. Your services, clients and bookings stay exactly where they are, so trying another look never means starting over.
