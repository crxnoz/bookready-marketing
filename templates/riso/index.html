The details

What makes Riso.

Every template runs the same booking engine: services, deposits, reminders, policies and reviews. Only the look changes.

A ghost on every headline.

Your name and every section title carry a 2px pink ghost shadow, the misaligned plate of a real risograph print, and the active tab gets one too.

Corner marks and cut lines.

Each section carries magazine page marks in IBM Plex Mono, and dashed cut-here rules with a scissors glyph run between panels, the layout language of a zine.

A two-ink cover.

Your cover photo prints as a true duotone, a gray plate overprinted in your accent ink, and the page closes with an END OF ISSUE sign-off.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I change the pink?

Yes. Choose Riso Pink, Citrus, Ocean, Forest or Press from your dashboard. The newsprint canvas and black ink stay fixed, so a new color reruns the whole issue in a fresh ink pair.

Is it only for hair studios?

No. It also suits tattoo artists, barbers and makeup artists with a print-shop eye. Every template has the same 12 sections, so rename and reorder the tabs to fit your work.

Can I switch later?

Yes, any time. Your services, clients and bookings carry over when you change templates, so trying a new issue never costs you a booking.
