The details

What makes The Fade Room.

Every template runs the same booking engine: services, deposits, reminders, policies and reviews. Only the look changes.

A real neon glow.

Eyebrows and section titles carry a soft glow in your accent color, and the buttons and active tab light up the same way, like a sign in the window.

Script like bent neon.

Dancing Script headings sit beside bold Bricolage Grotesque, a playful pairing that reads like hand-bent neon hanging over the mirror.

Rounded pills, soft corners.

Buttons are pill-shaped and cards have softly rounded corners, friendlier than a hard edge and confident enough for a shop that stays open late.

Who picks it

Barbershops with neon signage and late hours, men's grooming studios, boutique barber shops and solo barbers in a shared space pick it when they want the site to feel like the shop.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I change the neon?

Yes. Choose Hot Pink, Cyan, Lime, Purple, Sunset, Coral or White from your dashboard. The dark canvas and fonts stay fixed, so the shop keeps its after-hours feel whichever glow you run.

Does it work for nail or lash studios?

Yes. Nail and lash studios with after-hours energy fit it as well as barbers do. Rename and reorder the tab sections, and hide the ones your clients do not need.

Can I switch later?

Any time. Your services, clients and bookings stay put when you change templates, so a new look never means losing track of a single regular.
