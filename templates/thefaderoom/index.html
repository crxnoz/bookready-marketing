Built for the shop floor

A barbershop template that reads after-hours

The Fade Room is built for shops that earn their late hours. Near-black canvas, a single neon glow, deposits that hold, and a per-chair calendar for every barber. The Instagram bio link goes to a brand, not a form. Best for shops with a strong identity, an after-hours vibe, or a barber community that already sees the room as a destination.

On a phone

See it where your clients see it.

Already in the template

Every section a barbershop site needs

Hero with shop name and tagline. Service menu with prices and durations. Per-barber bios and chair calendars. A gallery for fades, beard sculpts, and color, with before-and-after toggles. Hours, location, and policies. Booking flow that takes a deposit at the time of booking. All of it pre-built, all of it on-brand. You change the text and the photos.

Who tends to pick it

Barbershops with a real identity

Shops with neon signage, late hours, and a culture around the work. Men’s grooming studios, boutique barber shops, and solo barbers in a shared space who want their site to read like the shop. Not a fit for old-school cash-only operations that do not take online bookings.

Plan that fits

Most shops on The Fade Room start on Studio

Studio at $29/mo covers a multi-chair shop with per-barber calendars, separate Stripe payouts, deposits, and a waitlist. Solo barbers can run The Fade Room on the Solo plan ($15/mo) with one calendar. Compare plans →
