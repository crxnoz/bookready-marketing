Built for clean rooms

A template for nail and skincare studios

Lush Studio reads cream and sage with handwritten script touches and soft, surface-forward photography. Designed for nail techs, estheticians, and small skincare studios that want a calm site that puts the work first. Deposits sized for hour-plus services, intake forms with allergies and contraindications, and a gallery filter built around square nail photography.

Already in the template

Every section a clean studio needs

Hero with studio name and a soft handwritten flourish. Service menu with add-ons (length, art, design tiers; intensity, peel level for skincare). Pre-visit intake form. Gallery with style filters and before-and-after toggles. Hours, policies, and aftercare instructions. Booking flow with deposits and recurring fills.

Who tends to pick it

Solo nail rooms and skincare studios

Solo nail techs running out of a clean home studio or a shared space. Estheticians doing facials, peels, and skin treatments in a treatment-room setup. Small brow studios with a soft brand. Not a fit for dark, dramatic moods, that is Velvet Theory.

Plan that fits

Solo covers a one-room nail or skin studio with the full booking flow, deposits, intake forms, and SMS reminders. Studio ($49/mo) adds per-staff calendars when you bring a second tech into the room. Compare plans →
