The details

What makes Lush Studio.

Every template runs the same booking engine: services, deposits, reminders, policies and reviews. Only the look changes.

An arch for your cover.

Your cover photo sits in a centered arch with a fully rounded top, resting on the cream canvas, with three small hearts drifting slowly up inside it.

Handwritten script titles.

Section titles carry a Cookie script flourish beside DM Serif Text, a handwritten touch that keeps the page friendly without losing its polish.

Hearts on the price list.

Service prices sit beside small hearts, a calm heart divider sits under your name, the tab pills stay soft and the Book button carries a gentle glow.

Who picks it

Solo nail techs in a clean home or shared studio, estheticians doing facials and peels in a treatment room, and small brow studios with a soft brand tend to pick Lush Studio.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I change the sage?

Yes. Choose Sage, Eucalyptus, Dusty Blue, Coral, Clay or Lavender from your dashboard. The cream canvas and the script fonts stay as they are, so the calm look holds whichever you pick.

Is it only for nails and skin?

No. Brow and lash studios with a gentle brand fit right in. Rename and reorder the tab sections so the menu matches what you offer, and hide the ones you skip.

Can I switch later?

Yes. Change templates any time from your editor. Your services, clients and bookings stay exactly where they are, so a new look never means a fresh start.
