The details

What makes Bleeker.

Every template runs the same booking engine: services, deposits, reminders, policies and reviews. Only the look changes.

A marquee that never stops.

A ticker bar scrolls between the main sections in your accent color, so the page keeps moving like a sign over the door. The active tab underline slides in to match.

Headlines like fly posters.

Massive uppercase Archivo Black headlines deliberately break the grid, with IBM Plex Mono labels beside them, like a poster wheat-pasted outside the shop.

Hard edges, square crops.

No rounded corners anywhere. Photos sit in square crops, and your studio card laps over the cover photo like a laminate stuck on a poster.

Who picks it

Barbers and barbershops that stay open late, late-night nail bars, tattoo studios with a loud brand and streetwear-aligned grooming brands tend to pick Bleeker.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I change the yellow?

Yes. Pick Safety Yellow, Ink Black, Chrome Silver, Hazard Orange, Court Red, Box Pink or Press Blue from your dashboard. The cream canvas and the fonts stay put, so it always reads as Bleeker.

Is it only for barbershops?

No. It also suits nail bars, tattoo studios and grooming brands with a loud voice. Every template has the same 12 sections, and you can rename and reorder the tabs to fit your menu.

What if I want a calmer look later?

Switch templates whenever you want. Your services, clients and bookings stay put, so going from loud to quiet never means rebuilding your site.
