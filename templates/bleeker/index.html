Built for the brand

A Bleeker template that reads its mood without effort

Bleeker runs like a downtown shop after midnight — yellow ticker overhead, marquee headlines, dashed cut-rules between sections. Built for shops that close late, take walk-ins, and care more about the lineup than the lobby decor. The whole page hums.

On a phone

See it where your clients see it.

Already in the template

Every section a Bleeker studio needs

Hero with the studio name in Archivo Black, ticker marquee at the top with promo copy that loops, sticky tab rail with a single-line underline, gallery with grid + strip layouts, results / before-after that read like fight posters, FAQ accordion in monospace, reviews stacked with a giant pull-quote, services menu that handles tiers + add-ons, full booking flow with deposits and Stripe Connect, footer with quick-book CTA and hours.

Who tends to pick it

Studios that match Bleeker’s mood

Barbers and barbershops that take walk-ins, late-night nail bars, tattoo studios with a queue at the door, streetwear-aligned grooming brands. Not a fit for hushed spa rooms or candlelit treatment studios — those want Arcanum or Opaline.
