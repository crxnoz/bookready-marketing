What makes Blush Gallery.

Every template runs the same booking engine: services, deposits, reminders, policies and reviews. Only the look changes.

Two prints, gently tilted.

The hero reads as two matted photo cards that fell against each other: your cover photo tilts one way and your portrait overlaps it, tilted the other.

Every photo matted.

Photos sit inside a cream mat behind a thin champagne-gold hairline, like prints mounted on a gallery wall, and the whole page keeps that framed logic.

A placard on the active tab.

The open tab gets a thin gold hairline frame around its label, like the small placard beside a piece in a show. No fills, no glow.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I change the colors?

Yes. Choose the canvas from your dashboard: Blush, Rose Petal, Mauve, Pearl or Cream. The champagne gold and cocoa ink never move, so all five looks still read as Blush Gallery.

I do nails, not lashes. Does it fit?

Yes. It was made for any pro whose photos sell the work: nails, lashes, brows and makeup. Rename the tab sections to match your services and reorder them to suit you.

Can I switch later?

Yes. Change templates any time from your editor. Your services, clients and bookings stay as they are, so a new look never touches your calendar.
