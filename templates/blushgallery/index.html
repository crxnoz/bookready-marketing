Built for portfolio work

A home for businesses that sell with photos.

Every gallery image sits in a mat with a hairline frame, so a phone snapshot and a studio shot both look considered. Generous margins keep sets from turning into a contact sheet. If your booking decision is made by looking at your last twenty sets, this is the template that shows them properly.

Already in the template

Every section a studio needs

Matted hero with a cover photo and an overlapping portrait. Service menu with add-ons and durations. Gallery and before-and-after in framed crops. Reviews, policies, hours, and a booking flow with deposits. Loyalty punch cards and gift cards ride along on every template.

Who tends to pick it

Soft, feminine, portfolio-first brands

Lash and brow studios. Makeup artists. Nail techs with a heavy gallery. Boutique beauty businesses that want pretty without going girly-cute. If your brand is moody and jewel-toned, Velvet Theory fits better. If you want clinical and photo-light, look at Clarity.
