Built for tattoo studios

A dark editorial home for the work.

Inkhouse runs warm charcoal with a rust default and a heavy Cormorant masthead. Built for fine-line, blackwork, and traditional tattoo studios, plus solo artists with a strong personal brand. The aesthetic is brutalist editorial without the grit, so the work stays the loudest thing on the page.

Already in the template

Every section a tattoo studio needs

Editorial hero with studio name and a single hero shot. Service menu styled like a print magazine. Per-artist bios with portfolio reels. Gallery laid out for full-bleed work shots. Hours, policies, deposit rules, and a booking flow with deposits. All of it dark, all of it considered.

Who tends to pick it

Studios with a dark, editorial brand

Fine-line and micro-tattoo studios. Blackwork artists. Traditional shops with a heritage feel. Solo tattooers with a strong personal brand. Piercers running alongside a tattoo room. Not a fit for warm, handcrafted studios, that is Bottega.
