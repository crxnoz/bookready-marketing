# BookReady changelog

BookReady uses **calendar versioning**: the version is `YYYY.MM`, the year and
month of the release. A `.PATCH` suffix marks additional updates within the same
month (for example `2026.06.1`). Because the version is the month, every shipped
update rolls it forward on its own, with no separate version to bump.

- **Current version: `2026.07`**
- This file is the canonical record. The public, human-facing version lives at
  [/changelog/](https://bkrdy.com/changelog/).

---

## 2026.07 (current)
- **Loyalty rewards.** A built-in, visit-based punch card. Owners choose how many
  visits earn a reward and what it is, every completed appointment counts
  automatically, and you can see who is enrolled, who has earned a reward, and
  redeem it in one tap.
- **One customer account across every BKRDY business.** Clients get a single login
  to manage appointments, view their history and receipts, and track loyalty
  progress across every business they book, and they control their own reminder
  preferences.
- A faster, full-screen sign-up and sign-in, plus a one-step setup that gets a new
  booking site live in minutes.

## 2026.06
- Six new templates: Blackline, Opaline, Petale, Bottega, Clarity, and Inkhouse.
  The library tripled to nine designed looks.
- Launched the Resources hub: Blog and Guides (pricing, deposits, no-shows,
  switching platforms).

## 2026.05
- **Soft launch.** The first three designed templates went live: The Fade Room
  (barbershops), Velvet Theory (lash and beauty), and Lush Studio (spas and
  estheticians). Booking, deposits, and reminders built in from day one.
