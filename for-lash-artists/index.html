How switching works

Questions

Short answers.

Can I take a deposit on lash sets?

Yes. Set one deposit rule, a flat amount or a percent of the price, and every booking asks for it. With a percent, a full set asks for more than a fill. It goes to your own Stripe account, and BKRDY takes no cut.

Can clients fill in an intake form?

Yes. Add your own questions to the booking form, like allergies or past reactions. Answers save to the client, so you have them at every visit.

I rent a room in someone else's studio. Does that work?

Yes. Your bookings, your clients and your Stripe payouts are yours, not the studio's, and they come with you if you move.

Does it connect to Instagram?

Your booking link works as your bio link, and the site is built to load fast from an Instagram tap. There is no direct Instagram integration.

Can I sell fill packages?

Yes, on paid plans. Bundle services into a package that clients pay for up front, then book against.

I'm on another app. How do I switch?

We move your services, clients and upcoming appointments for you, free, usually the same day. Your clients keep booking while we do it.
