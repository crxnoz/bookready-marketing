Designed first

A site that matches the room you actually work in

Three templates built for lash studios and solo artists. Velvet Theory runs deep burgundy with a constant warm gold, dramatic and after-dark. Opaline is pearl, marble, and soft champagne, the quiet luxury option. Petale is blush and rose with handwritten gold script. Pick the mood that matches your studio, add your work, go live.

Service-true durations

Booking that knows the trade

Full set, hybrid, classic, volume, mega volume. Refills at 2 and 3 weeks. Each priced and timed cleanly, with buffers built in for setup and breakdown. Clients see real openings, not estimated ones, so a 2-hour booking actually takes 2 hours and your next client is not waiting at the door.

Deposits, defended

New-client slots that don’t disappear

First-time clients pay a deposit before the booking holds. A 2-hour set is too valuable to lose to a no-show. Returning clients can skip the deposit if you want, the rule is set per service and per client type. The deposit becomes credit toward the appointment, so nothing feels punitive.

Intake on file

Aftercare, allergies, and notes that follow the client

Pre-visit intake form captures allergies, prior reactions, and any contraindications. Notes save to the client profile, so you have them at the next visit. Aftercare instructions send automatically after the appointment, with rebook reminders timed to the cycle (3 weeks for fills, 4 for refills).

Photo-forward

A gallery that does the selling

Built-in gallery with before-and-after toggles, optimized for mobile. Each photo can link to the exact service it shows, so a "Wispy Hybrid" on your gallery becomes a one-tap booking. Filter by style, by intensity, by curl. Photos load fast even on a slow phone.

Built for Instagram

A link that drops cleanly into your bio

Most of your traffic comes from an Instagram bio link. Custom domain (yourstudio.com) on Studio plan and up. Or a clean bkrdy.me subdomain free, like lash.bkrdy.me. Either way the page loads in under a second and the booking flow is built thumb-first.

Questions

The short answers for lash artists.

Can I require a deposit for new clients only?

Yes. Deposit rules are set per service and per client type (new vs returning), so returning clients can book without a deposit while new ones pay one.

How does the intake form work?

Clients fill it in before their first appointment, by clicking a link in the confirmation. Allergies, contraindications, and any notes save to their client profile so you have them next visit.

Can I sell aftercare kits through the site?

A basic product catalog ships in late 2026. For now, the gallery can link out to wherever you sell aftercare.

What if I work out of someone else’s studio?

BookReady works for solo lash artists in shared spaces. Your booking, clients, and Stripe payouts are yours, not the studio’s.

Does it integrate with Instagram?

There is no API integration, but your booking link works as your bio link, and the page is designed to load fast from an Instagram tap.

What about packages and series?

Pre-paid packages and series ship in late 2026. For now, individual services, deposits, and recurring fills are all wired in.

Can I block out time for personal stuff?

Yes. Block any time from the calendar in one tap, repeating weekly if you need (e.g. every Friday afternoon off).

How long does setup take?

Most lash artists are live in 20 minutes. Migration from Goldie, Acuity, or Booksy usually finishes the same day and is free.
