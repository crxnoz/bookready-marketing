Questions

The short answers for hair salons.

Can each stylist run their own commission split?

Yes. Each stylist has their own services, prices, and Stripe account (Studio plan and up). Commission tracking shows what each stylist earned per appointment.

How does color processing time work?

Services can include a processing block in the middle (e.g. 30 minutes of color, 45 minutes processing, 30 minutes finish). The stylist is free during the processing block to start another service or take a break.

What about double-booking (color processing + cut for another client)?

Yes. Each stylist can mark themselves "available during processing" and the calendar will book a second service during that block, with rules to prevent over-booking.

Can clients book a specific stylist?

Yes. The booking flow lets clients pick stylist first, then service. Or service first, then stylist. Or "any stylist." Whatever you want as the default.

How do I handle a stylist quitting or going on leave?

Block their calendar for the leave period. Existing bookings can be reassigned to another stylist or kept on hold. Their client list stays with the salon, not the stylist (by default, can be configured).

Does it handle gift cards?

Gift cards ship in late 2026. For now, the booking flow handles services, deposits, tips, and recurring appointments.

Can I do an "any stylist, soonest opening" booking?

Yes. "Any stylist" lets the system show the soonest opening across all qualified stylists.

How long does setup take?

A multi-stylist salon is usually live in under an hour, all services + stylists + hours + policies set up. Migration from Vagaro, Square, or Booksy usually finishes same day, free.
