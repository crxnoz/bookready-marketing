Questions

Short answers.

Can each stylist have their own calendar?

Yes. Add each stylist with the services they do and a login of their own. Studio covers up to six staff and Studio+ up to twelve, and you see every calendar side by side.

How does a long color appointment get booked?

Every service has its own length and buffer time before and after. A three and a half hour balayage holds that whole block on the calendar, and clients only see openings that fit it.

Does it track stylist commission or booth rent?

No. BKRDY handles bookings, deposits, tips and your client book. Payments go to your salon's Stripe account, and it does not split commission or collect booth rent.

Can I charge a deposit for color?

Yes. Set one deposit rule, a flat amount or a percent of the price, and every booking asks for it. With a percent, color asks for more than a blowout. Paid plans also keep a card on file and charge late cancellation fees.

Can I use my salon's own domain?

Not yet. Every BKRDY site lives at yourname.bkrdy.me today, a short link made for your bio and your texts. If your own domain matters to you, tell us at hello@bkrdy.com.

I'm on Vagaro. How do I switch?

We move your services, clients and upcoming appointments for you, free, usually the same day. Vagaro, Booksy, Square, GlossGenius and Fresha all work, and your clients keep booking meanwhile.
