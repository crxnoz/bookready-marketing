Designed first

A site that reads quiet

Three templates designed around how a spa feels at the door. Opaline runs pearl-and-marble with soft metallic accents. Petale is blush and rose with handwritten gold. Lush Studio is cream and sage with handwritten script. Pick the mood, the booking is already wired in.

Packages built in

Multi-service packages and add-on selling

A signature spa day bundle (massage + facial + aromatherapy) priced as one item, scheduled as three timed blocks. Add-on selling at booking: scalp massage, eye treatment, hot stones. Clients see the real total and the real duration before they hit book, no awkward surprises at checkout.

Per-room calendars

Practitioners, rooms, and chairs all have their own calendar

A single appointment can hold a practitioner and a room. If the massage therapist is available but the treatment room is not, the slot does not show. No double-bookings, no shuffling clients between rooms mid-visit, no calls about who is doing what when.

Series pre-pays

Deposits and series that fit spa pricing

A 3-hour spa day deserves a deposit. Set a flat amount, a percentage, or a per-hour rate. Series like a 6-session massage course or a body-treatment plan get paid up front and drawn down per visit, with remaining balance shown on the client profile and in confirmation emails.

Pre-visit prep

Reminders with the prep instructions baked in

A reminder that says "arrive 15 minutes early, drink water" lands better than a generic nudge. Pre-visit prep notes go out automatically with the reminder, 24 hours and 2 hours before the appointment. Aftercare instructions auto-send after the visit.

We import your services, hours, client list, and standing recurring bookings. Usually the same day, free, included on every plan. You keep your spa packages and your client database, so the first month feels seamless.

Questions

The short answers for spas.

Can I schedule by room, not just by practitioner?

Yes. Rooms have their own calendars. A booking holds the right practitioner AND the right room, so the slot only shows if both are free.

How do spa packages work?

A package is a bundle of services priced as one item. The booking flow schedules each service in sequence with the right buffer in between. Practitioners can be the same or different.

Can clients buy a series of treatments up front?

Yes. Series and packages are pre-paid, with sessions drawn from the balance. Remaining sessions show on the client profile and confirmation emails.

Does it handle gift cards?

Gift cards ship in late 2026. For now, the booking flow handles services, packages, deposits, tips, and recurring appointments.

Can I add product retail to the site?

A basic product catalog ships in late 2026. For now, gallery photos can link out to wherever you sell retail.

What about group bookings (couples massage, etc.)?

Couples and pairs bookings are supported by scheduling two services in the same time block with two practitioners and one room. We are shipping a dedicated group-booking flow in late 2026.

Can I limit a service to a specific practitioner?

Yes. Services can be assigned to specific practitioners, so clients only see openings with someone who actually provides that service.

How long does setup take?

Most spas are live in an hour with the full menu, hours, and team set up. Migration from Vagaro, Mindbody, or Square usually finishes the same day, free.
