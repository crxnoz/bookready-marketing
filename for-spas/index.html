Packages

Bundle the whole spa day.

Your team

Every practitioner, side by side.

Turnover

Time to reset between guests.

Add buffer time before and after any service for linens, cleanup or a slow start. Clients only see start times that really fit, so a 90 minute massage never crowds the next guest.

Questions

Short answers.

Can I schedule by room?

BKRDY books your practitioners, not rooms. Each person has a calendar, and buffer time before and after a service covers turnover. If you have fewer rooms than practitioners, stagger their hours to match.

Can I take deposits on long treatments?

Yes. Set one deposit rule, a flat amount or a percent of the price, and every booking asks for it. With a percent, a 3 hour package asks for more than a 30 minute facial. It goes to your own Stripe account, and BKRDY takes 0% commission.

Do clients get reminders?

Email confirmations and reminders go out on every plan. Paid plans add unlimited text reminders, and they keep a card on file so you can charge a late cancellation fee.

How many practitioners can I add?

Studio covers up to six staff and Studio+ up to twelve. Each person gets their own login and sees their own schedule, and every artist sits side by side on your calendar.

Can I sell gift cards and retail?

Gift cards, coupons and loyalty punch cards come with paid plans. The Shop lets clients reserve products and pick them up at the spa, so it suits a small retail shelf, not a full online store.

I'm on Vagaro. How do I switch?

We move your services, clients and upcoming appointments for you, free, usually the same day. It works the same from Booksy, GlossGenius, Square, Fresha, StyleSeat and Acuity.
