# BookReady version history

The full story of how BookReady has evolved, by version. We use **calendar
versioning** (`YYYY.MM`): the version is the year and month of the release, with
a `.PATCH` suffix for extra updates inside a month (for example `2026.06.1`).

This is the comprehensive reference, drawn from the product changelog and the
marketing-site commit history. For the short public list, see
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) and [/changelog/](https://bkrdy.com/changelog/).
For what is coming, see [/roadmap/](https://bkrdy.com/roadmap/).

Two tracks run in parallel:
- **Product** — the BookReady app owners use (booking, payments, templates).
- **Site** — bkrdy.com, the marketing site, launched May 31, 2026.

---

## 2026.06 — current
The biggest month yet. The template library tripled, and the marketing site
grew a full content and SEO engine.

**Product**
- Six new templates: Blackline, Opaline, Petale, Bottega, Clarity, and Inkhouse.
  Nine designed looks in all.

**Site**
- Resources hub launched: Blog and Guides.
- Automated content engine: real keyword research (DataForSEO) feeds an
  approve-then-publish queue that writes articles, generates on-brand hero
  images, ships them, and emails a daily digest.
- SEO program: industry pages, competitor comparison pages, two-way internal-
  linking clusters, rich schema (Organization, WebSite, fixed breadcrumbs),
  an llms.txt for AI assistants, and 301 redirects for legacy URLs.
- Performance: oversized images resized, fonts loaded async, gzip and cache
  headers added (faster mobile LCP).
- GA4 analytics wired into every page.
- A welcome page for early members.
- Adopted calendar versioning and this version history.

## 2026.05
**Product**
- Soft launch. The first three designed templates went live: The Fade Room
  (barbershops), Velvet Theory (lash and beauty), and Lush Studio (spas and
  estheticians), with booking, deposits, and reminders built in.

**Site**
- The BookReady marketing site went live (May 31): image-rich homepage with an
  interactive booking demo, full mega-menu navigation, capability-gated pricing
  with annual billing and an SMS calculator, and the Platform and Features
  sections (31 pages).

## What's next

A phased journey (see [/roadmap/](https://bkrdy.com/roadmap/) for the full view):

- **Phase 1 — Business essentials (in build):** group and recurring bookings, packages, gift cards, Reviews 2.0, SMS + calendar integrations, email and SMS campaigns, SEO tools.
- **Phase 2 — Revenue growth:** memberships, loyalty, referrals, commerce, Availability 2.0.
- **Phase 3 — Mobile ecosystem:** iOS and Android business apps.
- **Phase 4 — Discovery network:** a customer-facing discovery and rewards layer.
- **Phase 5 — Business growth platform:** marketing suite, analytics, AI features, deeper integrations.
- **Phases 6 to 7 — Community and Marketplace:** the long-term vision.
