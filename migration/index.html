Questions

Migration, answered.

How much does migration cost?

Nothing. Free migration is included with any BookReady plan. We rebuild your services and import your client list at no extra charge.

Will my clients notice?

No. You keep your old platform running until your BookReady site is live, so there’s no gap in bookings. Most migrations finish the same day.

What exactly do you move over?

Your services and pricing, your client list with contact details, your business hours, and your staff. Card details aren’t transferred for security, clients re-add a card at their next booking.

How long does it take?

Once we have your export file, most migrations are done the same day. Larger multi-staff salons can take a little longer, and we’ll tell you up front.

Do I have to cancel my old platform first?

No, keep it running until you’re happy with your BookReady site and taking bookings. Then cancel the old one whenever you’re ready.

What about StyleSeat?

StyleSeat locks your client data in and has no clean export. We know how to work around it, email us and we’ll walk you through it before you even sign up.
