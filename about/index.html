Who’s behind it

Made by a studio, not a startup factory.

BookReady is a product of DaysGraphic LLC — a small design studio that got tired of watching talented beauty pros hide behind ugly booking links and clunky scheduling apps. So we built the booking site we’d want for our own chair, and made it something a one-person studio can actually afford.

The short version

Booking software forgot how to look good.

The big platforms treat your booking page like a form. Same gray layout for a luxury lash studio and a tire shop. We think the place your clients book should feel like the work you do — considered, specific, yours. BookReady is the result: real designed templates, a serious booking engine underneath, and pricing built for the people actually doing the work.

What we believe

A few things we won’t budge on.

Design isn’t a luxury

Every plan gets the full template library — the same designed sites, deposits, and reminders. We don’t hide good taste behind a higher tier.

Core belief

Your clients are yours

Your client list, your bookings, your brand. Export it whenever you want. We’re a tool you rent, not a wall you’re locked behind.

Core belief

Software should disappear

The best booking flow is the one your client doesn’t notice. Fewer taps, no account walls, no confusion — just a time picked and a confirmation sent.

Core belief

Priced for one chair

You shouldn’t need a salon’s budget to look professional. BookReady starts at a price a solo barber or lash artist can sign up for on a Tuesday and not think twice.

Core belief
