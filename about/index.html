The short version

Booking software forgot how to look good.

The big platforms treat your booking page like a form. The same gray layout for a luxury lash studio and a tire shop. We think the place your clients book should feel like the work you do: considered, specific, yours.

So BKRDY starts with a designed site, not a widget. Fifteen templates, each with the same twelve sections in its own style, and a real booking engine underneath: deposits on your own Stripe account, reminders, policies and a calendar that keeps every artist in view.

The calm way to run a busy book means fewer tabs and fewer surprises. Clients book at 2 AM without making an account. You open your day and see what is next, who has a balance due and what needs you.

What we believe

A few things we won't budge on.

Your work deserves a real site.

Every plan, Free included, gets a designed booking site at yourname.bkrdy.me. Free starts with four templates, paid plans open all fifteen, and switching templates keeps your services, clients and bookings.

Your clients are yours.

Your client list, your bookings, your brand. Download your clients and appointments as a CSV file any time. Payments land in your own Stripe account, and BKRDY takes 0% commission on them.

Booking should feel quiet.

Clients book as a guest, with no account and no password. They get a confirmation with a link to cancel or reschedule inside your windows, and a reminder before they come in.

Priced for one chair.

You should not need a salon budget to look professional. Free is $0 forever with no card. Solo is $21 a month for one calendar, and every paid plan includes unlimited text reminders.

Kito

Meet Kito, your assistant.

Kito is the assistant inside your editor. Ask him about your calendar, your clients or your money, and he answers in plain words with real names, times and numbers. He finds openings, and he can tell you how busy the book has been and what sells.

When something should change, like a new booking, a move or a blocked afternoon, Kito puts it on a card. Nothing happens until you tap Confirm. He works for you only: Kito never talks to your clients.
