Why BKRDY

Beauty businesses are some of the tightest communities there are.

Community starts with individuality. Show what your brand is about. That’s what BKRDY is for.

Questions

The short answers.

Do I need a card to start?

No. The Free plan is $0 forever with no card required, so you can get a real booking site live today. When you pick a paid plan you start a 30-day trial with your card on file. You’re only charged when the trial ends, and you can cancel from your dashboard before then.

Can I bring my own domain?

Custom domains come with our Custom Websites service, a fully custom site built for you on the BKRDY engine. Free, Solo, and Studio plans get a clean BKRDY subdomain like yourname.bkrdy.me.

What about transaction fees?

BKRDY doesn’t take any. You pay Stripe’s standard rate (2.9% + 30¢ in the US) and that’s it. Deposits and full payments go straight to your Stripe account.

Can I switch templates after I sign up?

Anytime. Switching keeps your business info, services, bookings, customers, staff, hours, and policies. Only the look changes.

Will these prices go up?

These are early-access founding-member prices. Lock in your rate now and it stays put for as long as your subscription stays active, even as we add features and raise prices for new customers.

Is BKRDY built for the US?

Right now, yes. Payments are Stripe US and the booking flow assumes US business hours. International expansion is on the roadmap.
