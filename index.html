Questions

The short answers.

What happens after the 30-day trial?

You pick a plan and your card is charged. If you don’t want to continue, you can cancel from your dashboard and your site stays live for 30 days while you export your data.

Can I bring my own domain?

Yes, on Studio and Salon plans. Solo gets a clean BookReady subdomain like yourname.bkrdy.me. Studio and Salon let you point a custom domain.

What about transaction fees?

BookReady doesn’t take any. You pay Stripe’s standard rate (2.9% + 30¢ in the US) and that’s it. Deposits and full payments go straight to your Stripe account.

Can I switch templates after I sign up?

Anytime. Switching keeps your business info, services, bookings, customers, staff, hours, and policies. Only the look changes.

Will these prices go up?

These are early-access founding-member prices. Lock in your rate now and it stays put for as long as your subscription stays active, even as we add features and raise prices for new customers.

Is BookReady built for the US?

Right now, yes. Payments are Stripe US and the booking flow assumes US business hours. International expansion is on the roadmap.
