Frequently asked

What happens after the 14-day trial?

You pick a plan and your card is charged. If you don’t want to continue, you can cancel from your dashboard and the site stays live for 30 days while you export your data or migrate.

Can I bring my own domain?

Yes — on Studio and Salon plans. Solo gets a clean BookReady subdomain like yourname.bkrdy.me; Studio and Salon let you point a custom domain.

What about transaction fees?

BookReady doesn’t take any. You pay Stripe’s standard rate (2.9% + 30¢ in the US) and that’s it. The deposits and full payments your customers make go straight to your Stripe account.

Can I switch templates after I sign up?

Anytime. Switching templates keeps your business info, services, bookings, customers, staff, hours, and policies. Only the look changes.

What if I run out of SMS in a month?

You’ll get a heads-up email at 80% used. On Studio and Salon, additional SMS are auto-billed at $0.025 each (with a hard cap you set). On Solo, you can buy a top-up pack or upgrade to Studio — whichever costs less.

Is BookReady built for the US?

Right now — yes. SMS is 10DLC US-only, payments are Stripe US, and the booking flow assumes US business hours. International expansion is on the roadmap but not live yet.
