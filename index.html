Questions

Short answers.

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. A live booking site and calendar at $0, with no card and no time limit. Upgrade only when you want text reminders, staff calendars or the extras.

Do you take a cut of my bookings?

Never. Payments go to your own Stripe account. Stripe charges its standard card fee, and BKRDY adds nothing on top.

What is Kito?

Kito is the AI assistant inside BKRDY. He answers questions about your calendar, clients and money, finds openings, and lines up bookings, moves and time off for you. Nothing changes until you tap Confirm.

Does Kito talk to my clients?

No. Kito works for you, inside your BKRDY editor. Your clients never see him.

I already use another app. Is switching a pain?

No. We move your services, clients and upcoming appointments for you, free, usually the same day.

Is BKRDY available outside the US?

Not yet. Payments run on Stripe US, so BKRDY is for US businesses for now. More countries are on the roadmap.
