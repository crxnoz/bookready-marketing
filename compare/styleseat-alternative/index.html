How switching works

Questions

Short answers.

Does BKRDY charge clients a booking fee?

No. Clients book on your site as a guest and pay only what you set: your deposit, or the full price if they choose. BKRDY takes 0% of your bookings, and card payments carry Stripe's standard fee.

What does StyleSeat charge for new clients?

StyleSeat's help center lists its optional New Client Connection fee at 30% of a new client's first appointment, with a $50 maximum. It applies once per client, only to clients StyleSeat sends you.

Can you move my StyleSeat book?

Yes, for free. We move your services, clients and upcoming appointments for you, usually the same day. Then you swap your bio link to your BKRDY site.

Do I get my own website?

Yes. Every plan includes a designed booking site at yourname.bkrdy.me, with your gallery, policies and reviews.

What does BKRDY cost?

Free is $0 forever, with no card. Solo is $21 a month, or $18 billed yearly, with every template and unlimited text reminders. Paid plans start with a 7-day trial.
