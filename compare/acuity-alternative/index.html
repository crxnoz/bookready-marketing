Acuity wins

When Acuity is the right call.

No platform is right for every business. Here is where Acuity is genuinely stronger today.

You book across many industries

Acuity is genuinely generic.

If you run sessions across very different categories (beauty + coaching, or therapy + classes), Acuity is more flexible. BookReady is built specifically for beauty businesses, and we lean into that. If your business is half something else, Acuity stretches further.

You live in the Squarespace ecosystem

Native Squarespace integration.

If you already run on Squarespace and want to keep that, Acuity is now Squarespace-owned (since 2019) and integrates natively. You can keep your Squarespace site and add Acuity scheduling without rebuilding. BookReady replaces both.

You need deep integrations

Acuity has a mature API and Zapier presence.

Acuity has been around since 2011 and built out 1,000+ integrations through Zapier. If you have a custom workflow that depends on connecting your scheduler to a CRM, an email tool, and three other apps, Acuity is more battle-tested. BookReady has the integrations most beauty businesses actually use (Stripe, Google Calendar, Apple Calendar) and adds new ones based on real customer requests.

Your Acuity export gives us your services, hours, staff, and client list. We rebuild it inside BookReady and walk you through the new template. Usually the same day, included on every plan, no setup fee. You stay on Acuity until you decide to flip your Instagram bio link.

Questions

The short answers about switching.

Can I move my Acuity calendar and client list to BookReady?

Yes. Acuity exports your appointments, services, and client list as CSV. We import all of it on your behalf during free migration. You keep every name, email, phone number, and standing recurring booking.

Do I need to keep Squarespace if I move from Acuity to BookReady?

No. BookReady includes the website, so you can cancel Squarespace too. Most studios save $20+/mo just from that consolidation, on top of whatever they save on the BookReady vs Acuity comparison.

Does BookReady support class booking like Acuity?

Class booking (group sessions with multiple attendees) is on the roadmap for Q1 2027. If most of your revenue is class-based today, Acuity is the stronger pick. If most of your revenue is one-on-one appointments, BookReady handles that better.

What about Acuity intake forms?

BookReady has intake forms for new clients (allergies, prior reactions, contraindications, custom fields you define). They save to the client profile so you have them at every visit. Same as Acuity, just baked into the booking flow.

Does BookReady have an API?

BookReady has a more focused integration set today (Stripe, Google Calendar, Apple Calendar). If you need a custom integration that does not exist, contact us. We are building integrations based on real customer needs, not building a thousand and hoping ten get used.

Will I get charged twice during the switch?

No. BookReady offers a 30-day free trial with no card for the first 7 days. Cancel Acuity on your own timing, after BookReady is live and you have confirmed bookings are landing.

Can I keep my Acuity booking link active during the switch?

Yes. Acuity keeps your link live until you cancel. We set up your BookReady site (custom domain or bkrdy.me subdomain) before you switch your bio link. You decide when to flip.

How much does BookReady save me vs Acuity + Squarespace?

On the entry plans: Acuity Emerging ($20/mo) + Squarespace Personal ($16/mo) = $36/mo. BookReady Solo = $15/mo. Save $21/mo, plus you keep your time managing one product instead of two.
