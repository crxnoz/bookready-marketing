Short answer

Which one should you pick.

GlossGenius is a strong choice if you need a full POS with inventory tracking, native mobile apps, or multi-location support. BookReady is the better choice if you want your website and your booking to be one thing instead of two, and you do not want to pay 2.6% per transaction. Most solo and small-studio beauty pros end up picking BookReady for the designed templates alone.

GlossGenius wins

When GlossGenius is the right call.

No platform is right for every business. Here is where GlossGenius is genuinely stronger today.

Retail product as a meaningful revenue line

Full POS with hardware and inventory.

If retail (skincare, hair products, jewelry, candles) is a meaningful part of your monthly revenue, GlossGenius has chip readers, register hardware, and live inventory tracking. BookReady has Stripe checkout for services. A basic product catalog ships in late 2026, but it will not replace a register.

Multi-location chains

More than one physical location.

If you run a chain with multiple physical locations under one brand, GlossGenius handles that as a first-class feature. BookReady multi-location support is on the roadmap for Q1 2027. For a single salon or studio, this does not matter. For a 3-location chain, it does.

Native owner app

iOS and Android apps for daily use.

If you live in your booking platform's app, GlossGenius's native iOS and Android apps are more polished than BookReady's mobile web. BookReady is web-first by design (no install, no compatibility drift), but if a native app is non-negotiable for how you work, GlossGenius is stronger today.

Questions

The short answers about switching.

Can I move my GlossGenius client list to BookReady?

Yes. GlossGenius exports client contacts as CSV, and we import them on your behalf during the free migration. You keep every name, email, and phone number.

Do I lose my GlossGenius online booking link when I migrate?

No. We set up your BookReady domain (or a bkrdy.me subdomain) before you switch your bio link. You decide when to flip. The GlossGenius link goes offline only when you cancel.

What happens to my reviews on GlossGenius when I migrate?

Reviews collected on GlossGenius live in their system. We help you re-engage your client list on BookReady so new reviews start collecting on your site. We do not migrate reviews directly because of GlossGenius data policies.

Does BookReady support tipping like GlossGenius?

Yes. Tipping at checkout is built into the BookReady booking flow, with custom percentages, a no-tip option, and per-staff tip routing if you are on Studio plan.

Can I run a salon with multiple stylists on BookReady?

Yes, on the Studio plan at $29/mo. Each stylist gets their own calendar, services, and separate Stripe payouts.

Will I get charged twice during the switch?

No. BookReady offers a 30-day free trial (no card for the first 7 days). Cancel GlossGenius on your own timing, after you have switched your bio link and confirmed bookings are landing.

Does BookReady have a POS for in-person checkout?

BookReady checkout runs through Stripe in your browser. You can use Stripe tap-to-pay on iPhone or Stripe Reader hardware. If you need a full POS with cash drawer and chip reader, GlossGenius is a stronger fit today.

How does BookReady’s 0% fee actually work?

BookReady takes 0% of your transaction. Stripe still charges their standard processing fee (about 2.9% + 30 cents per transaction). Most other platforms (including GlossGenius) stack a markup of 1-3% on top of Stripe. BookReady does not. On $5,000 per month in bookings, that saves you about $130 per month.
