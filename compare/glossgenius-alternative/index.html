Questions

Short answers.

Does BKRDY have a card reader?

No. BKRDY takes payments online through your own Stripe account: deposits, full prepayment, balances and tips. If you mostly take cards in person, GlossGenius's readers may suit you better.

Can you move my GlossGenius clients?

Yes, for free. We move your services, clients and upcoming appointments for you, usually the same day, so your booked clients keep their times while you switch.

Is the BKRDY website really designed?

Yes. You pick from designed templates, 4 on Free and all 15 on paid plans, each with booking, gallery, results, about, policies, reviews and more. Switching templates keeps your services, clients and bookings.

What does BKRDY cost for a team?

Studio is $34 a month, or $29 billed yearly, for up to 6 staff. Studio+ is $55, or $47 yearly, for up to 12. Each artist gets their own login and sees their own schedule.

What is Kito?

Coming soon. Kito is your AI assistant inside BKRDY. He answers questions about your calendar, clients and money, and proposes changes as a card you confirm. He never talks to your clients.
