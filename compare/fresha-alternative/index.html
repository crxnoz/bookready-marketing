vs Fresha

Fresha alternative. Your brand, not theirs.

Fresha is free, which is great, until you realize the trade-off. Your clients book through fresha.com, see Fresha branding everywhere, and live inside Fresha's app afterward. The relationship is theirs, not yours. BookReady gives you your own designed website at your own domain, 0% platform markup, and a brand that is actually yours. Here is how the two compare.

Short answer

Which one should you pick.

Fresha is the right choice if you want $0 entry, are happy with marketplace exposure, and do not mind your clients booking under the Fresha brand. BookReady is the better choice if you want to own your brand, own your client relationship, and have a designed website that is yours, not theirs. Most beauty pros end up choosing brand ownership.

BookReady wins

When BookReady is the right call.

Own your brand

Your website, your domain, your brand on every page.

A Fresha profile says Fresha at the top, in the URL, in the search bar, and in the app store. Your clients book on Fresha, with Fresha branding, and the next time they want to book something else, they come back to Fresha, not to you. BookReady gives you a designed website at your own domain. Your brand is on every page. Period.

Own your client relationship

Your clients are yours, not the marketplace's.

Fresha is a marketplace. The traffic is theirs. The client relationship is shared. The day Fresha changes their algorithm or pricing, your business changes too. BookReady has no marketplace. Your clients come from your Instagram, your word of mouth, your work. That relationship is yours forever.

0% platform markup

Save the 1% on every booking.

Fresha takes 1% of every card payment (or 1.69% via Fresha Pay). BookReady takes 0%. On $5,000/mo in bookings, that is $50/mo Fresha keeps. BookReady costs $15/mo and keeps $0. Net: you save money on bookings above ~$1,500/mo, and you get a designed website instead of a marketplace profile.

Designed website included

A real website, not a marketplace profile.

Fresha gives you a generic Fresha-branded layout, the same one every Fresha business uses. BookReady gives you Velvet Theory, The Fade Room, Opaline, or one of four others, designed for beauty businesses. The first impression is your brand, not the platform.

Fresha wins

When Fresha is the right call.

No platform is right for every business. Here is where Fresha is genuinely stronger today.

You want $0 entry

Fresha is genuinely free upfront.

If cash flow is tight and you need to start charging clients before you can afford a subscription, Fresha's $0 entry is real. You pay the 1% per booking, no monthly fee. BookReady starts at $15/mo. For solos doing under ~$1,500/mo in bookings, Fresha is technically cheaper.

You want marketplace traffic

Fresha is a real client acquisition channel.

Fresha lists ~23,000 salons globally, and people search "salons near me" on fresha.com directly. If you can stand the marketplace exposure and want a real channel for finding new clients (especially in tourist-heavy areas), Fresha's marketplace is a genuine asset. BookReady does not have one and does not plan to.

You operate globally

Fresha is multi-currency and multi-region.

If you run a salon in London, Sydney, or Madrid, Fresha is built for that, with multi-currency support, multi-language, and local payment methods. BookReady is United States only today.

Questions

The short answers about switching.

Can I move my Fresha client list to BookReady?

Yes. Fresha exports clients as CSV. We import every name, email, phone number, and booking history on your behalf during free migration.

What happens to my Fresha profile when I migrate?

It stays live as long as you keep your Fresha account active. You can keep both running during the switch (Fresha for marketplace traffic, BookReady as your own website), then close Fresha when you are ready.

How do I get clients to book on BookReady instead of Fresha?

Switch your Instagram bio link, your Google Business Profile booking link, and your email signature to your BookReady URL. New clients book there. Old clients see the new link and follow it.

Does BookReady have a marketplace like Fresha?

No. We are not building one. Your clients find you through your own marketing (Instagram, word of mouth, Google), and the relationship is yours. If marketplace exposure is critical to your business, Fresha is the better choice.

Will I lose my Fresha reviews?

Reviews collected on Fresha stay in Fresha. We help you re-engage your client list on BookReady so new reviews start collecting on your site. We do not migrate reviews directly because of Fresha data policies.

Does BookReady work for non-US businesses?

Today, BookReady is United States only (payments are Stripe US). International expansion is planned but not committed to a date. If you are outside the US, Fresha or a local platform is a stronger fit today.

Will I get charged twice during the switch?

No. BookReady offers a 14-day free trial with no card for the first 7 days. Fresha is $0/mo so there is nothing to double-bill. Run both in parallel for a week, then switch your bio link.

How does the 0% vs 1% math actually work out?

Fresha: $0 + 1% of bookings. BookReady: $15/mo + 0%. Break-even is $1,500/mo in bookings. Below that, Fresha is cheaper. Above that, BookReady is cheaper AND you own your brand AND get a designed website. Most established beauty pros are above the break-even within 3-4 months.
