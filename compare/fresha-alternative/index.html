Questions

Short answers.

Does BKRDY have a marketplace like Fresha?

No. BKRDY is your own booking site, not a directory of studios. Clients reach you through your link and your bio, and you pay 0% commission on every booking they make.

What does Fresha charge for new clients?

Fresha's pricing page lists a one-time 20% commission, with a $6 minimum, on brand new clients who find you through its marketplace. Returning clients and direct booking links are free.

Can you move my Fresha data?

Yes, for free. We move your services, clients and upcoming appointments for you, usually the same day, so your booked clients keep their times while you switch.

Is BKRDY free?

BKRDY Free is $0 forever with no card: a booking site, deposits, a client book and email reminders. Solo is $21 a month and adds every template and unlimited text reminders.

How do payments work on BKRDY?

Payments go to your own Stripe account. Take a deposit at booking, a flat amount or a percent of the price, plus balances, tips and refunds. BKRDY takes 0%, and Stripe's standard 2.9% + 30¢ applies.
