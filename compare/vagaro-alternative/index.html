Website

Deposits

Deposits without an extra cart.

Questions

Short answers.

What does BKRDY cost compared with Vagaro?

BKRDY Free is $0. Solo is $21 a month for one calendar, and Studio is $34 for up to 6 staff. Vagaro's pricing page lists $30 a month for one calendar, plus $10 per added calendar.

Does BKRDY charge extra for the website?

No. Every plan includes a designed booking site. Free uses 4 templates and a yourname.bkrdy.me address, and paid plans get all 15 templates.

Can you move my Vagaro data?

Yes, for free. We move your services, clients and upcoming appointments for you, usually the same day, so your booked clients keep their times while you switch.

Does BKRDY have a marketplace?

No. BKRDY is your own site, so clients book you directly and you pay 0% commission on every booking. There is no list of other studios next to yours.

Can I see BKRDY bookings in my own calendar?

Yes. Subscribe to your private, read-only calendar feed in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. Busy times in those calendars do not block your booking slots, so block time in BKRDY.
