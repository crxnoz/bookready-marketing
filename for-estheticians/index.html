How switching works

Questions

Short answers.

Can I collect allergies and consent before the visit?

Yes. Add your own questions to the booking form, like allergies, past reactions and consent. Answers save to the client, so you have them at every visit. You write the questions, so they fit your treatments.

Can I sell a series of treatments?

Yes, on paid plans. Bundle services into a package that clients pay for up front, like a series of peels or facials, then book their visits against it.

Can I require a patch test before booking?

You can add a patch test question to the booking form and mark it required, so a client cannot book without answering it. You see the answer before the visit. BKRDY does not verify the test itself.

Do reminders go by text or email?

Both. Email confirmations and reminders are on every plan, and text reminders are unlimited on every paid plan. Clients can cancel or reschedule from their email, inside the windows you set.

Can I sell my retail skincare?

Yes. Add your retail products to your Shop. Clients reserve them online and pick them up from you, so there is nothing to ship.

I'm on another app. How do I switch?

We move your services, clients and upcoming appointments for you, free, usually the same day. We work from Booksy, Vagaro, GlossGenius, Square, Fresha, StyleSeat and Acuity.
