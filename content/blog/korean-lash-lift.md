Clients are asking for the Korean lash lift by name. US searches for it have roughly tripled over the past year, and many of the people searching already know the look they want: lashes lifted from the root, straight and natural, without the tight curl of an older lift.

If you already do lifts, this is a variation on a service you know. If you don't, it is a new service with its own training. Either way, the money is in how you name it, price it and book it.

## What a Korean lash lift is

A lash lift is a semi-permanent treatment that lifts and shapes the natural lashes, with no extensions involved. What lash suppliers call a Korean lash lift is mostly a difference in sequence and result.

In a traditional lift, the lashes are usually stuck to a curved shield before the first solution goes on. In the Korean approach, the lashes are generally softened first and set in place afterwards, often against a flatter shield. The result clients are after is lashes that look lifted from the base and straight, not curled.

The product is not what makes it Korean. Both styles can use different formulas, so the name describes the method and the look rather than a single brand.

## Korean lash lift vs regular lash lift

| | Korean lash lift | Regular lash lift |
|---|---|---|
| The look | Lifted from the root, straighter, open | A visible curl |
| The order | Lashes softened, then set in place | Lashes placed on a curved shield first |
| Shield | Often flatter | Curved, chosen for the curl |
| The skill | Judging when the lashes are ready | The same, plus choosing the curl |

Neither one is automatically gentler. A careful traditional lift can be kinder to the lashes than a rushed Korean one. What protects the client's lashes is the processing check, and that is the part training teaches.

## Train before you sell it

Learn it from a course or from the maker of the system you will use, and practice on models before you charge full price. A lift that processes too long can leave lashes frizzy or brittle, and a client remembers that longer than any good set.

Follow your training and your product's instructions for the consultation, patch testing, timing and how long to wait between lifts. Ask every client about recent lash services, extensions and anything going on with their eyes before you start, and be ready to say no.

## How long a Korean lash lift lasts

A lift lasts until the lifted lashes shed and new ones grow in on their own cycle. Lash suppliers usually put that at around six to eight weeks, and it varies from client to client. Book the next lift around the interval your product recommends, not sooner, because lifting too often is hard on the lashes.

## Put it on your menu the way clients search

Call it a Korean lash lift. That is the phrase people type, and a menu that only says "lash lift" makes them wonder whether you do this one.

Describe the result in plain words, say how long it takes and how long it lasts, and say what is included. If you offer a tint with it, make the tint a separate choice so clients can see the price of each.

:::copy Menu description for a Korean lash lift
Korean lash lift. Your natural lashes lifted from the root for an open, straight look, no extensions and no mascara needed. About [time]. Lasts around six to eight weeks as your lashes grow. Add a tint for darker lashes.
:::

On BKRDY, a tint can be set up as an add-on to the lift service, so clients choose it while they book and the time and price adjust on their own.

## How to price it

Work from your own numbers: the products for one lift, the time it takes you including the consultation, and what you need to earn per hour. Then look at what you charge for your current lift. If the Korean lift takes you longer or the system costs more, the price should say so.

Check what studios near you charge, but do not copy the lowest price. Clients asking for this by name are looking for someone who does it well. The same sum in [how to price nails as a beginner](/blog/how-to-price-nails-as-a-beginner/) works for any service.

## The trade-off

Trends cool. Training and a new system cost money before the first client books, so add it if clients are asking you for it, not because it is everywhere online. Keep your regular lift on the menu for clients who want the curl.

A lift also costs less than a full set of extensions. If some of your extension clients switch to lifts, your bookings can rise while your average ticket falls. That can still be a good trade, since a lift is a shorter appointment, but count it before you decide.

## Questions

### What is a Korean lash lift?

A lash lift that softens the natural lashes before setting them in place, usually for a straighter lift from the root instead of a tight curl. No extensions are involved.

### What is the difference between a Korean lash lift and a regular lash lift?

Mostly the order of the steps and the look. A regular lift places the lashes on a curved shield first and gives a visible curl. A Korean lift softens the lashes first and aims for a straighter, lifted look.

### How long does a Korean lash lift last?

Until the lifted lashes shed and new ones grow in, usually around six to eight weeks depending on the client's natural lash cycle.
