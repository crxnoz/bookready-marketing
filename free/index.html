Questions

Free, answered honestly.

Is BKRDY actually free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 forever, not a trial. No card, no expiry date, no booking caps. We make money when businesses outgrow Free and upgrade for premium features.

Do I need a credit card to sign up?

No. You sign up with Google or email, pick a template, and your site is live. A card only ever comes into it if you choose a paid plan later.

Can I take payments and deposits on the Free plan?

Yes. Connect your own Stripe account and take deposits or full payments at booking. BKRDY adds no transaction fee; you pay only Stripe’s standard rate and payouts go straight to your bank.

Is there a limit on appointments?

No. Unlimited appointments on every plan, including Free.

What’s the catch?

Free includes 4 of our designed templates instead of the full library, keeps a small Powered by BKRDY badge on your site, and leaves out premium features like text reminders, gift cards, coupons, loyalty rewards, and after-hours premium slots. The booking engine itself is never gated.

What happens if I upgrade later?

Everything unlocks immediately and your site, services, clients, and bookings stay exactly as they are. Downgrading back to Free keeps your site live too.

Switching from another platform?

Coming from GlossGenius, Acuity, Square, Fresha, Booksy, or Vagaro. We rebuild your services, hours, staff, and client list. Free, included on every plan. You keep everything that matters.
