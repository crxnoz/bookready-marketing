How switching works

Questions

Free, answered honestly.

Is BKRDY really free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 for good, not a trial. No card, no end date and no booking cap. We earn when a studio wants the paid extras and upgrades.

Do I need a card to sign up?

No. Sign up with Google or email, pick a template and your site is live. A card only comes in if you choose a paid plan later, which starts with 7 days free.

Can I take deposits on Free?

Yes. Connect your own Stripe account and take a deposit at booking, a flat amount or a percent of the price, or let clients pay in full. BKRDY adds no fee, and Stripe's standard rate applies.

Is there a limit on appointments?

No. Appointments are unlimited on every plan, Free included.

What's the catch?

Free has 4 templates, keeps a small BKRDY badge on your site, and leaves out text reminders, card on file and extras such as the waitlist, gift cards and loyalty. The booking engine is the same on every plan.

What happens if I upgrade, or come back to Free?

Upgrading starts with 7 days free, and everything unlocks right away. Your site, services and clients stay as they are. If you cancel later, your site stays live on Free.
