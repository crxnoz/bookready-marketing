How switching works

Questions

Short answers.

Do I need a website if I have Instagram?

Your bio link sends followers somewhere they can book at any hour. A designed site with booking built in also shows your work, prices and policies in one place. There is no direct Instagram integration.

Can I mix different kinds of services?

Yes. Every service has its own length, price, photo and category, so a cut, a lash fill and a facial can sit on one menu. Each one can have its own deposit.

Which templates are on the Free plan?

Four: Opaline, Blackline, The Fade Room and Lush Studio. Paid plans include every template, and they start with a 7-day trial that needs a card.

What does 0% commission mean?

BKRDY takes 0% of every booking. Payments go straight to your own Stripe account, and Stripe's standard card fee applies. BKRDY adds nothing on top of it.

Can I take time off without losing bookings?

Yes. Add time off for any dates and clients stop seeing openings then. Ask Kito who is already booked and he proposes the moves as a card. Nothing changes until you tap Confirm.

I'm on another app. How do I switch?

We move your services, clients and upcoming appointments for you, free, usually the same day. It works from Booksy, Vagaro, GlossGenius, Square, Fresha, StyleSeat and Acuity.
