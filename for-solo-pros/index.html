Designed first

A real website that reads professional

Solo pros usually get one of two things online: a free booking widget that looks like every other free booking widget, or a Squarespace site with a booking button bolted on. Both feel like compromise. BookReady gives you a designed site (Velvet Theory, Opaline, The Fade Room, or any of the others) WITH the booking already wired in. One site, one experience.

One calendar, one set of rules

Your hours, your services, your way

You set the hours you actually work, the services you actually offer, and the policies you actually enforce. No staff to manage, no permissions to figure out. Block off time for personal stuff in one tap. Reschedule everything for a week in two. The whole calendar is yours.

Deposits, defended

A no-show on a solo booking hurts more

When it is just you, every no-show is the whole day, not just one chair. Deposits sized to the service, enforced at booking time. Cancellation window and late-cancel fee built into the flow, not on a paper sign. Solo pros usually see no-show rates cut in half in the first month.

Reminders that hit

Reminders so clients show up

Email confirmation immediately after booking, then email reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before the appointment, plus aftercare instructions auto-sent after the visit. SMS text reminders are coming soon. You do not chase no-shows. The platform handles the reminders, you do the work.

Questions

The short answers for solo beauty pros.

Do I really need a website if I have an Instagram?

Yes. A booking link in your bio sends the follower somewhere they can actually book. A booking widget is not a brand. A designed website with booking built in is your brand AND the booking, in one place.

Can I switch templates later if I get tired of one?

Yes. Switch templates from your dashboard and keep every service, client, booking, deposit policy, and gallery photo. Only the look changes.

Does it work for makeup artists who travel?

Yes. Services support "travel to client" location with a travel-fee add-on. The booking flow asks for the address before it holds the slot.

What if I’m not technical?

Most solo pros are live in 20 minutes. The setup is pick template, add services, set hours, connect Stripe, share the link. No code, no plugins, no dev.

Can I block out a week for vacation?

Yes. Block any range in one tap. Vacation block auto-replies to booking requests with a message you set ("back on March 15, message to be added to the waitlist").

How does the Solo plan handle taxes?

Stripe handles the transaction. Tax tracking and reporting integrations ship in late 2026. For now, your Stripe dashboard shows everything you need for quarterly filings.

Will it work with my custom domain (yourname.com)?

Custom domains are on the Studio plan and up. Solo gets a free bkrdy.me subdomain (yourname.bkrdy.me) which is clean and shareable.

What does the 0% fee actually mean?

BookReady takes 0% of your transactions. Stripe still charges their standard processing fee (about 2.9% + 30¢), but BookReady does not stack a markup on top. Most booking platforms charge 1-3% on top of Stripe. We do not.
