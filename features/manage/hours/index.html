Good to know

Hours questions.

Can each person have different hours?

Yes — everyone sets their own working hours, breaks, and days off, all independent of each other.

How do I handle a vacation?

Block the dates and the calendar simply won’t offer them. Clients never see your time off as bookable.

Can I add cleanup time between clients?

Add a buffer to a service and the calendar leaves that gap automatically around every booking.

Why it matters

No more 6am booking requests.

Nothing’s more annoying than a booking request for a time you don’t work — a Sunday, a lunch break, the week you’re away — that you now have to decline and apologize for.
