Why it matters

No-shows cut roughly in half.

For a small studio, a no-show isn’t a small thing. It’s an empty chair you can’t refill on short notice and an hour you’ll never bill, and on a busy week, a couple of them is real money gone. Most of the time it isn’t a flake, either; it’s someone who genuinely forgot.

A confirmation the moment they book and a friendly nudge the day before fixes most of that. And because every message carries a one-tap reschedule link, the client who truly can’t make it moves the slot instead of vanishing, which means you can still fill it.

  • Email reminder 24 hours before (SMS coming soon)
  • One-tap reschedule keeps the slot fillable
  • Fully automatic, nothing for you to send
