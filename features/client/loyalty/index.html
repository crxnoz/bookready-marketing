How it works

Set it once, then forget about it

You pick how many visits earn a reward and what the reward is. That is the whole setup. Everything after that runs on its own.

1

Choose your reward

Every 5th visit, every 10th, whatever suits you. Write the reward in your own words: a free fill, a complimentary add-on, half off a set.

2

Visits count themselves

Every completed appointment adds a stamp automatically. No app for your client to download, no card to lose, nothing for you to remember.

3

You get told when it is time

When someone earns a reward they move to the top of your loyalty list. Give it to them, tap Redeem, and their card starts fresh.

What is new

Now it shows up everywhere they look

A reward nobody can see does not bring anyone back. So we put the card in front of your clients at every moment that matters.

📧

In their confirmation email

Every booking email can carry their punch card, so they see how close they are the moment they book. You choose: every booking, only when they are nearly there, or never.

📱

On their booking page

When a client opens their appointment details, their stamps are right there, along with a friendly nudge to make a free account and keep track of their rewards.

✨

On your website

Turn on a sweet little band that tells first-time visitors your regulars get rewarded. It picks up your template colours automatically, and it is off until you say so.

✍️

Stamped by hand when you need to

Someone paid cash, or you forgot to close out an appointment? Add a visit yourself with one tap. Took one off by accident? Take it back the same way.

In their inbox

The nudge that actually works

Most clients never make an account anywhere. That is fine. Their card rides along in the email they already open.
