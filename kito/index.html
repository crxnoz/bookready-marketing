Questions

Kito, answered.

What is Kito?

Kito is the AI assistant inside BKRDY. He answers questions about your calendar, clients and money, and lines up changes for you: bookings, prices, your website, your hours, a pay link for what a client owes. Nothing changes until you tap Confirm.

Can Kito change things without asking me?

No. Every change arrives as a card that says exactly what will change, who gets an email and what happens to money. Nothing happens until you tap it, and you can always say never mind.

Can Kito refund or charge a client?

No. He never refunds and never charges a card. He tells you what was paid and what is owed, and he can email a client a link to pay. The decision and the money stay with you.

Does Kito talk to my clients?

No. Kito works for you, inside your editor and the BKRDY app, and your clients never see him. An email he lines up, like a confirmation or a pay link, is the same one your studio already sends, and it goes out only after you tap.

What can Kito see?

Your studio’s calendar, menu, site, rules and what each booking cost and still owes. When he looks up a client he gets a first name and a last initial, not their email, phone number, private notes or card.

How many messages do I get?

25 a month on the Free plan and 100 a month on Solo, Studio and Studio+. A message is one thing you ask him.
