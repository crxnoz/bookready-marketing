Changelog · v2026.07

What we’ve shipped.

The short version of how BKRDY keeps getting better. We version by the month (v2026.07 is this July), newest first.

Jul 2026

New

Loyalty rewards, built in

  • A visit-based punch card: choose how many visits earn a reward and what the reward is. Every completed appointment counts automatically, nothing to stamp by hand.
  • See who’s enrolled, who’s earned a reward, and redeem it in one tap.

Jul 2026

New

One account, every BKRDY business

  • Your clients get a single BKRDY login to manage appointments, view their history and receipts, and track loyalty progress across every business they book, with no new password for each one.
  • Clients control their own reminder preferences.

Jul 2026

Improved

A faster way to get started

  • A simpler, full-screen sign-up and sign-in, plus a one-step setup that gets your booking site live in minutes.

Jun 2026

New

Verified reviews, and four more templates

  • Reviews: after a visit, clients can leave a star rating and a note. You approve what goes public, and approved reviews show right on your site.
  • Four new designs, Bleeker, Chroma, Riso, and Arcanum, bring the library to 13.

Jun 2026

New

Scheduling on your terms

  • A smart calendar with date drops, open availability on your own rhythm instead of all at once.
  • A waitlist that fills cancellations automatically.
  • After-hours and squeeze-in slots for premium, last-minute requests.
  • Per-day and per-staff capacity so you never get overbooked.

Jun 2026

Improved

Your bookings, your brand

  • Redesigned bookings: a day and week calendar, plus an appointment drawer that keeps every action in one place.
  • A new Introduction band, greet visitors with your team, a highlight reel, a countdown, or a welcome offer.
  • Content maker turns any service into a ready-to-post social graphic.
  • Set your own currency and time zone (38 supported), and start with a free plan, no card required.

Jun 2026

New

Six new templates and the Resources hub

  • The template library tripled: Blackline, Opaline, Petale, Bottega, Clarity, and Inkhouse, for nine designed looks in all.
  • Launched Blog and Guides, practical reads on pricing, deposits, no-shows, and switching platforms.

May 2026

Launch

Soft launch: our first three templates

  • The Fade Room, dark and bold for barbershops.
  • Velvet Theory, deep and jewel-toned for lash and beauty.
  • Lush Studio, soft and editorial for spas and estheticians.
